Action films are a genre of film that typically feature high-energy sequences, intense physical stunts, and fast-paced storytelling. These films often revolve around a hero or protagonist who faces various challenges, obstacles, and adversaries, often involving combat, chases, explosions, and other thrilling elements. Action films are designed to keep audiences on the edge of their seats, providing excitement and adrenaline-pumping entertainment.
