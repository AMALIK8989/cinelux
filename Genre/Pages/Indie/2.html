Indie Movies: Frequently Asked Questions

What are indie movies?

Indie movies are generally films made outside, or with greater independence from, the major mainstream studio system. They often feature distinctive creative approaches, personal storytelling, or less conventional subjects.

How do I find a movie on this page?

Browse the indie movie cards and select the film icon on a card to open its dedicated page. Movie-specific details may vary by title.

Does every title have the same information?

No. Details depend on each individual movie page. Open a title to check the information provided for that film.
