Gray Matters (2006)

About the Film

When Love Changes the Equation

Gray Matters follows Gray Baldwin (Heather Graham) and her brother Sam (Tom Cavanagh), two siblings whose closeness often makes strangers mistake them for a couple. Hoping to expand their romantic lives, they agree to set each other up. Gray introduces Sam to Charlie Kelsey (Bridget Moynahan), and the connection between them is immediate. But as Sam and Charlie rush toward marriage, Gray realizes that her own attraction to Charlie is more than friendship. The resulting romantic comedy explores self-discovery, family bonds, unexpected jealousy and the courage to be honest about who you are.

Written and directed by Sue Kramer, the film blends New York romance, screwball-style banter and a coming-out storyline. It premiered at the Hamptons International Film Festival in October 2006 before its limited US theatrical release in February 2007.

Gray and her brother Sam decide to find each other partners. When Sam falls for Charlie, Gray discovers that her own feelings for Charlie are more complicated than she expected, prompting her to confront her identity.

Gray Matters was written and directed by Sue Kramer.

The principal cast features Heather Graham as Gray Baldwin, Bridget Moynahan as Charlie Kelsey and Tom Cavanagh as Sam Baldwin. The supporting cast includes Molly Shannon, Rachel Shelley, Alan Cumming and Sissy Spacek.

The film premiered at the Hamptons International Film Festival on October 21, 2006, and opened in limited US theaters on February 23, 2007.
