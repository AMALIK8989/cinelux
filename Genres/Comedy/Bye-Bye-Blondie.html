About the Film

A Second Chance at a First Love

Gloria and Frances had a summer romance in their teenage years. In their late forties they meet again and attempt to retrieve the romance they had in their youth. The film is set against the punk culture of the early 1980s and the teenage revolt that fuels its story, themes that run through Despentes' work, including sexual freedom and nightlife.

Adapted from Despentes' novel, the film was made as her second feature after Baise-moi, and stars Béatrice Dalle as Gloria and Emmanuelle Béart as Frances. Soko and Clara Ponsot play the two women as teenagers.
