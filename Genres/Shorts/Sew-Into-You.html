Sew Into You2023

NR Short Film Romance 11m

A widowed older woman named Maggie has never dated a woman before, so she turns to her younger queer coworker Orion for advice — a little courage, a handwritten note, and the nerve to finally ask someone out. Sew Into You is a warm, funny, festival-winning short about the idea that there's no "correct" way to be queer, and no wrong age to start.

About the Film

A First Date, Decades in the Making

Writer-director Emma Josephson made Sew Into You with her real-life partner, co-writer Tara Umesh, drawing on their own six-plus years together as a queer couple. At a craft shop, widowed Maggie (Jane Ferguson) admits to her younger coworker Jean (Jennifer Lanier) that she'd like to ask out a woman she's noticed — she's just never done it before. Orion (Jacqueline Mai) becomes an unlikely dating coach, and the film unfolds as a gentle, playful lesson in flirting: a note left on a windshield, a heart drawn in the corner, the terror of writing down a phone number. Rather than leaning on the age-gap romances so common in queer film and TV, Sew Into You is deliberately about a relationship between two women past 50 — proof, as Josephson has said, that there's no single "correct" way to be queer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sew Into You is a short film running about 11 minutes. It premiered at the Seattle Queer Film Festival and the Lower East Side Film Festival NY in 2024.

Sew Into You was directed by Emma Josephson, who co-wrote it with Tara Umesh, and stars Jane Ferguson as Maggie, Jennifer Lanier as Jean, and Jacqueline Mai as Orion.

A widowed older woman named Maggie seeks guidance from her younger queer coworker, Orion, to ask out a woman for the first time — a warm story about becoming comfortable in your own queerness at any age.

Sew Into You was filmed in Portland, Oregon, and won Best Oregon Film at the 2024 McMinnville Film Festival. It also screened at the Tacoma and Bushwick Film Festivals.
