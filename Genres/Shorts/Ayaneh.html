About the Film

A Chance Meeting at the Pool, and the Freedom It Awakens

Living in Switzerland for eight months with her mother and older brother after fleeing Afghanistan, Ayaneh spends her days at the public pool wrapped in a burkini, watching the other swimmers with something between curiosity and longing. When an outgoing Swiss girl named Anna introduces herself, an instant spark forms between them — one that pulls Ayaneh toward a version of herself she's never been allowed to explore. As their bond deepens, Ayaneh must weigh her family's religious expectations and her own safety within her community against a desire she can no longer suppress, in a story about rebelling against cultural norms and the cost of choosing yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Ayaneh is a short film, running approximately 14 minutes. It had its World Premiere at the Rhode Island International Film Festival in 2019.

The film was written and directed by Nicolas Greinacher, and stars Afsaneh Dehrouyeh as Ayaneh, Ladina von Frisching as Anna, Mo Ahmadi as Haroun, and Mithra Zahedi as the mother.

Set in Switzerland, Ayaneh follows a young refugee from Afghanistan who meets a local girl named Anna at a public swimming pool and feels an instant attraction to her — a connection that puts her at odds with her religious family's expectations.

Ayaneh was filmed in Switzerland and had its World Premiere at the Rhode Island International Film Festival in August 2019, before screening at over 30 international festivals including NewFest and Holebi.
