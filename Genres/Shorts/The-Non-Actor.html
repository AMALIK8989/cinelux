About the Film

An Unexpected Connection

Elliot is staying with her ex’s new girlfriend while attending a medical trial for her recent, unexplained loss of hearing. As she reconfigures her relationship with her sonic environment and herself, an intimate connection sparks between the two women. Directed by Eliza Callahan, this intimate short drama explores change, vulnerability and the ways people learn to connect.
