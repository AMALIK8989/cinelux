About the Film

One Ex, One Deadline, and a Very Queer New Year's Eve

After a disastrous speed-dating night crystallizes just how alone she feels heading into her 30s, Jenn gets an unexpected wake-up call: a chance at getting her ex-girlfriend Frankie back before the ball drops. With her friend Annie roped in as reluctant wingwoman, Jenn throws herself into a series of increasingly chaotic schemes to win Frankie's heart, all while fending off her mother's video-chat check-ins and her own creeping certainty that she's running out of time. Written, directed by, and starring Breanne Williamson, Aging Out is a warm, funny holiday rom-com built specifically for a queer audience that rarely gets one of its own — proof, as Williamson puts it, that "life just doesn't end. You keep getting to rediscover yourself."

Frequently Asked Questions

Aging Out is a short film, running approximately 42 minutes. It had its World Premiere at the Rio Theatre in Vancouver on October 17, 2023.

The film was written, directed by, and stars Breanne Williamson as Jenn, alongside June Laporte as Annie, Kaila Hildebrandt as Danny, Carolyn Yonge as Lydia, and Iris Rhian as Frankie.

Just months before Jenn's 30th birthday, she is dumped by her long-term girlfriend Frankie. With the help of a surprise houseguest, Jenn sets off on a mission to win Frankie back before the new year.

Aging Out was written, filmed, and produced in Vancouver, British Columbia, and released to the public on YouTube on December 16, 2023, following its Rio Theatre premiere.
