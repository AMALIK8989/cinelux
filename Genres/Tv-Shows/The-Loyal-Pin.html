About the Series

A Bond Across a Lifetime

After losing her parents, Mom Rajawongse Pilanthita — Khunying Pin — is taken in by her aunt, Princess Pattamika, and raised at the Lotus Palace, where she grows close to Her Serene Highness Aninlaphat, Princess Anin. When Anin turns fifteen, her family sends her to study abroad, and the two stay connected only through letters across the years. When Anin finally returns home, old affection resurfaces — but between royal duty, family expectation, and a suitor already pursuing Pin's hand, the two women must decide what their bond is truly worth fighting for.
