About the Series

Before He Was Superman

In October 1989, a meteor shower devastates the small town of Smallville, Kansas — and with it arrives a young boy who will be adopted by Jonathan and Martha Kent and named Clark Kent. Twelve years later, Clark struggles to understand his growing powers and his place in the world, forming an unlikely friendship with a young Lex Luthor after saving his life, and falling for his childhood crush Lana Lang. Alongside best friends Chloe Sullivan and Pete Ross, Clark navigates high school, first love, and the secrets that come with being different, all while facing meteor-infected villains and uncovering the truth of his own origins on his long road toward becoming the hero the world will one day call Superman.
