About the Series

Rivals by Day, Secret Lovers by Night

Enemies With Benefits (Thai: ลัลล์ไม่ชอบไวน์) is a 2026 Thai Girls' Love romantic comedy from GMMTV and Snap25. Lal, a driven Head of Sales, is desperate to outshine Wine, the feared Head of Accounting. An unexpected night turns them into secret friends with benefits, yet at the office they have to keep up the rivalry so no one finds out.

As the arrangement continues, the bickering softens and real feelings appear. The series blends workplace comedy, enemies-to-lovers tension and heartfelt moments, starring Jan Ployshompoo Supasap and JingJing Prariyapit Yu in their first leading pairing.

Adapted from Snow Leopard's novel, it premiered on GMM 25 on 3 May 2026 and ran weekly for 10 episodes, followed by the special Married to My Enemy. It is a standalone story set in the same universe as Whale Store xoxo. If you enjoy office romances and slow-burn rivals-to-lovers, also explore Frozen Valentine and Heart Code.

Enemies With Benefits (Thai: ลัลล์ไม่ชอบไวน์) follows Lal, Head of Sales, and Wine, Head of Accounting, two office rivals who fall into a secret friends-with-benefits arrangement. They must keep pretending to be enemies at work, and their closeness slowly turns into real love.

Ployshompoo Supasap (Jan) plays Lal and Prariyapit Yu (JingJing) plays Wine. The supporting cast includes Ploynira Hiruntaveesin, Rutricha Phapakithi, Phatchatorn Thanawat, Chayakorn Jutamas, Juthapich Indrajundra, Kittipat Chalaragse, Niti Chaichitathorn, Leo Saussay and Rattanawadee Wongthong.
