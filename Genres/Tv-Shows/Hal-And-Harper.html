About the Series

Comfort and Curse

Hal and Harper have built their adult lives side by side in Los Angeles, tethered by a lifetime of inside jokes and shared pain. When the two return to their childhood home — now up for sale — their father delivers news that upends everything they thought they understood about their family. Told through nonlinear storytelling and flashbacks that weave between childhood and the present, the series traces how the trauma of their upbringing shaped the codependent, sometimes suffocating bond between them, and asks what it would take for either of them to finally grow up on their own terms.
