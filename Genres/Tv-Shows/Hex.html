About the Series

The Dark Secrets of Medenham Hall

Hex is a British supernatural drama centered on the strange history of Medenham Hall, a boarding school built on a site connected to centuries of supernatural activity. The first season follows Cassie Hughes, a shy student whose discovery of her family's supernatural heritage draws her into a dangerous relationship with forces she does not fully understand. Her friendship with Thelma Bates becomes increasingly important as the two encounter the mysterious Azazeal and uncover the secrets hidden beneath the school's seemingly ordinary surface. The second season expands the mythology of Medenham Hall and introduces a new central storyline involving Ella Dee and Malachi, continuing the series' exploration of supernatural power, temptation, relationships, and the consequences of ancient forces entering the modern world.

Hex is a British supernatural drama set at the mysterious Medenham Hall. The story initially follows Cassie Hughes, who discovers a supernatural family legacy and becomes connected to Thelma Bates and the powerful Azazeal.

Hex has two seasons and 19 episodes in total. Season 1 contains six episodes, while Season 2 contains thirteen episodes.

The principal cast includes Christina Cole as Cassie Hughes, Jemima Rooper as Thelma Bates, Michael Fassbender as Azazeal, Jamie Davis as Leon Taylor, Amber Sainsbury as Roxanne Davenport, and Colin Salmon as David Tyrel.

Hex originally aired in the United Kingdom on Sky One from 2004 to 2005 and ran for two seasons.
