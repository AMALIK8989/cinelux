About the Series

Every Ghost Story Is a Love Story

In 1987, American au pair Dani Clayton arrives at Bly Manor, a sprawling English country estate, to care for two young orphans, Miles and Flora, following the mysterious death of their previous governess. Welcomed by housekeeper Hannah Grose, groundskeeper Owen, and gardener Jamie, Dani soon senses something is deeply wrong within the manor's walls — flickers of a presence that was never meant to leave. As Dani grows closer to the children and to Jamie, she is drawn deeper into Bly's tangled past, uncovering a tragic history of obsession, grief, and love that refuses to let go, even in death.
