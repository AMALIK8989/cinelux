About the Series

A Poet Ahead of Her Time

Budding writer Emily Dickinson uses her outsider's perspective to explore the constraints of society, gender, and family in the 19th century. Blending period drama with a distinctly modern voice, the series follows Emily's private world of ambition, love, and verse against the rigid expectations of Amherst society.
