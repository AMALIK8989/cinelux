About the Series

A Marriage of Convenience, A Love That Never Left

In Love Forever (วาดฝันวันวิวาห์) is adapted from the Thai web novel Wat Fan Wan Wiwa by reallyb. Ran (Sirilak Kwong), a sharp and successful lawyer, and Neen (Kornnaphat Sethratanapong), a headstrong Gen-Z heiress, once built a life together — until relentless pressure from Ran's controlling mother, Kingkamol, tore their marriage apart. Years later, a high-stakes business deal forces the two ex-wives to appear as a happily married couple once again. Living under the same roof, maintaining the illusion becomes harder than either expected, as unresolved feelings, old wounds, and long-buried resentments rise back to the surface. Surrounded by deception, family expectation, and a love neither of them ever truly let go of, Ran and Neen must decide whether their broken marriage deserves a second chance — or whether some scars simply never heal.
