About the Series

Farm Life and the Fate of the World

Clark Kent and Lois Lane return to Smallville with their twin sons, Jonathan and Jordan, where they are reacquainted with Lana Lang, her husband Kyle Cushing, and their daughter Sarah. Away from the pressures of Metropolis, Clark hopes to give his boys a more grounded upbringing — but as the family settles into life on the Kent farm, new threats emerge that force Clark to confront what it means to be both a father and Superman at the same time. Balancing marriage, parenthood, and the weight of protecting the world, the Kent family faces enemies old and new across a saga that closes out the story that began generations before them.
