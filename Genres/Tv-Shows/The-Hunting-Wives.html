About the Series

Welcome to Maple Brook

Sophie O'Neil moves with her husband Graham and their son from Cambridge, Massachusetts to Maple Brook, deep in East Texas, hoping to leave a traumatic past behind and start fresh. She quickly falls into the magnetic orbit of Margo Banks, the wife of oil tycoon Jed Banks and the ringleader of a tight-knit clique of elite housewives. What begins as harmless fun — parties, guns, and gossip — soon turns into something far more dangerous, as an obsessive connection between Sophie and Margo pulls Sophie deeper into a world of seduction, jealousy, and secrets the town would kill to protect.
