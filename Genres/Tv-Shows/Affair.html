About Affair the Series

Affair the Series is an authentic Thai GL (Girls' Love) romance drama released in 2024 with 8 episodes on iQIYI. The series features lead actors Punyapat Wangpongsathaporn (as Wan/Lookmhee) and Saranphat Pedersen (as Siangphleng/Sonya). The narrative centers on childhood friends who reunite after a decade of separation caused by family bankruptcy, exploring themes of lost love, class conflict, and emotional reunion. The cast includes Pop Pataraphol Wanlopsiri, Apasiri Nitibhon, and Bonz Nadol Lamprasert in supporting roles. Production credits: iQIYI / Studio Atmosphere.
