About the Series

Filling in the Blank Between Two Hearts

Blank The Series (Thai: เติมคำว่ารักลงในช่องว่าง) is a 2024 Thai Girls' Love romance from Nine Star Studios, adapted from Chao Planoy's novel. Khun Nueng (M.L. Sipakorn), played by Faye Peraya Malisorn, is a refined woman of royal lineage who has never been allowed to be herself. When she meets Anueng (Yoko Apasra Lertprasert), whose smile hides a quiet sadness, her guarded world begins to change.

Season 1 follows their growing bond and the obstacles of a sixteen-year age gap, family expectations and social norms, and it ends on a cliffhanger. Season 2 picks up the fallout, with Anueng now at university and both women forced to decide what they are willing to fight for.

The series shares a universe with GAP The Series, reimagining characters such as Nueng, Sam and Mon with a new cast. If you like emotional age-gap romances, try Enemies With Benefits or Frozen Valentine next.

Blank The Series (Thai: เติมคำว่ารักลงในช่องว่าง) follows Khun Nueng, a woman of royal lineage raised under her grandmother's strict expectations, who has never felt truly loved. Her heart stirs when she meets the cheerful Anueng, but a sixteen-year age gap and family and social pressure stand in the way.

Faye Peraya Malisorn plays Nueng and Yoko Apasra Lertprasert plays Anueng.
