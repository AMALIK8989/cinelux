About the Series

A New Blueprint for Love and Ambition

Architect Aokbab returns from Japan to help rescue her family’s struggling firm. To compete for a major renovation project, she builds a new team and approaches Rin, an acclaimed independent architect who has always worked on her own. As the two women learn to collaborate, professional disagreements give way to a growing connection. But the return of Mind, Aokbab’s first love from ten years earlier, brings old feelings and fresh complications into both work and romance.
