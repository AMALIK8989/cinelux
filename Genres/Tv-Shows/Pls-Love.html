About the Series

When Pride Meets an Old Flame

Pls Love (Thai: PLS Love รักได้ไหม) is a 2026 Thai Girls' Love romance from Channel 3 and BEC World, adapted from Reverse's novel. Pheem (Phakinee Traisuwetthiwat), played by Lena Lorena Schuett, is a composed and successful business heiress. Parn (Parnupsorn), played by Miu Natsha Taechamongkalapiwat, is the woman from her past who now works as a hostess at an exclusive Bangkok club.

Determined to keep Parn close, Pheem hires her as her personal assistant. Working side by side across corporate offices, luxury hotels and Bangkok nightlife forces the two to confront pride, painful misunderstandings and the class difference between their worlds. The series blends romance, comedy and drama over 8 episodes.

It is the second on-screen pairing of Lena and Miu after My Safe Zone. If you enjoy tense, slow-burning GL romances, also try Blank The Series and Enemies With Benefits.
