About the Series

Two Sisters, One Secret

Ai-oon has always been the disappointing twin beside her successful, beloved sister Ob-oom. On the night of Ob-oom's wedding, she pulls Ai-oon aside with an unusual request: end the affair she's been secretly having with a woman named May before she leaves for her honeymoon. But when a tragic accident kills Ob-oom's husband and leaves Ob-oom comatose, Ai-oon must carry out her sister's wishes alone — only to discover that May is blind, and unaware that the sister she's falling for isn't the one she thinks. As Ai-oon steps deeper into her sister's identity, admiration curdles into something far more complicated, and the lines between duty, guilt, and love blur beyond recognition.
