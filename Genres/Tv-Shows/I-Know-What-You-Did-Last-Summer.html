About the Series

Someone Remembers

A year has passed since a graduation-night celebration ended in a fatal car accident on the cliffs above their island town. The group of friends who covered it up thought their secret died with the truth — until a killer in a fisherman's slicker starts picking them off one by one, each attack a message that someone knows exactly what they did. As twin sisters Allison and Lennon and their friends race to uncover who's hunting them, they're forced to confront the buried resentments, betrayals, and secrets that have been quietly poisoning their friendships since long before that night — and to realize that in this town, everyone is guilty of something.
