About the Series

The Hood of Star City

Billionaire playboy Oliver Queen is missing and presumed dead after his family's yacht, the Queen's Gambit, sinks in a violent storm. Five years later, he's found alive on a remote island — and returns home to Starling City a changed man, hardened by everything he survived. Determined to fulfill his father's dying wish and right the wrongs of his family's past, Oliver secretly becomes the hooded vigilante the press calls "The Hood," using a list of names to target the corrupt individuals destroying his city. As he builds Team Arrow with allies like John Diggle and Felicity Smoak, Oliver's double life as billionaire and vigilante collides again and again with the people he loves — across eight seasons that would ultimately give rise to the Arrowverse.
