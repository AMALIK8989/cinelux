About the Series

An Unlikely Mentorship

Legendary Las Vegas comedian Deborah Vance is watching her empire shrink. When she reluctantly hires Ava Daniels, a young, canceled writer, to punch up her material, the two clash — and slowly, unexpectedly, begin to understand each other, redefining what success, identity, and relevance mean at very different stages of a career.

Frequently Asked Questions

Hacks is a comedy-drama series that follows Deborah Vance, a Las Vegas comedic legend, and Ava Daniels, a young writer, as they join forces in a mentorship that challenges both of their definitions of success, identity, and relevance.

The main cast includes Jean Smart as Deborah Vance, Hannah Einbinder as Ava Daniels, alongside Paul W. Downs, Megan Stalter, and Carl Clemons-Hopkins.

Hacks first premiered on May 13, 2021.
