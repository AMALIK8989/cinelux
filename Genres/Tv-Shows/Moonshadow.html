About the Series

A Deal That Was Never Just a Deal

Moonshadow (Thai: เงาใต้พระจันทร์, also known as A Shadow Underneath the Moon) is a 2026 Thai Girls' Love romantic drama from GMMTV and Nar-ra-tor, adapted from Flower of Memory's novel. Chan (Sun), played by Emi Thasorn Klinnium, has no plans to continue after a one-night stand with Key, played by Bonnie Pattraphus Borattasuwan. Charming and mischievous, Key proposes something bolder: that Chan keep her as her "kept girl".

The arrangement gives Chan rules that feel safer than romance, but they slowly fail as real feelings grow. Then Jay (Film Rachanun Mahawan), Chan's first love, reappears, and the new relationship has to survive the old one. The 10-episode series blends mature romance, humour and emotional drama, and is directed by Fon Kanittha Kwunyoo.

It is the second on-screen pairing of Emi and Bonnie after Us (2025). If you enjoy slow-burning, grown-up GL romances, also try Pls Love and My Safe Zone.

Frequently Asked Questions

Moonshadow (Thai: เงาใต้พระจันทร์) begins when Chan has a one-night stand with Key. Chan wants nothing more, but the mischievous Key proposes to be her "kept girl". The unconventional arrangement slowly becomes real love, until Jay, Chan's first love, reappears and shakes everything.

Emi Thasorn Klinnium plays Chan (Sun) and Bonnie Pattraphus Borattasuwan plays Key. The cast includes Film Rachanun Mahawan as Jay, Kay Lertsittichai as Phu, Jamie Juthapich Indrajundra as Peach, AJ Chayapol Jutamas as Game, Pete Thongchua, Supranee Charoenpol, Sarocha Watittapan, Apasiri Nitibhon and Duangta Toongkamanee.

Moonshadow has one season of 10 episodes, and all 10 are listed on this page.

It premiered on 12 August 2026 and aired weekly on Wednesdays on GMM 25, with the finale on 14 October 2026. It also streamed on oneD.

It is produced by GMMTV with Nar-ra-tor and directed by Fon Kanittha Kwunyoo. The screenplay is by Lalil Kittitanaphan and Naphat Chitveerapat, with Sataporn Panichraksapong as executive producer.
