About the Series

Love Through the Airwaves

Sleep With Me is a Filipino GL romance drama about Harry, a wheelchair-bound radio DJ who offers relationship advice to broken hearts. When Luna meets Harry through the airwaves, their connection deepens into something profound. Together with their circle of friends, they navigate love, healing, and the transformative power of human connection in this heartfelt six-episode series.
