About the Series

When Memory Is Not the Truth

Third Person (Thai: ถ้อยรัก...คำลวง) is a 2026 Thai Girls' Love mystery romance from North Star Entertainment, adapted from SIIX's web novel and directed by Arisa Wawwanjit. Chris, played by Garn Nuttacha Ratchayangkanont, lost her memory in an accident four years ago and has since lived peacefully with her loving partner Nadia, played by Piglet Charada Imraporn.

Everything changes when Sun, played by Mimie Bhapat Ahchariyasripong, enters Chris's life. Chris feels drawn to her, and fragments of her past begin to return. As long-hidden secrets surface, she is forced to ask whether the life she rebuilt was built on lies, and whether her relationship is heading for disaster.

The 10-episode series blends romance, mystery, a love triangle and emotional drama. It aired on Channel 3 on Saturday nights from 12 September to 21 November 2026. If you like emotional twists in GL romances, also try Moonshadow and Pls Love.
