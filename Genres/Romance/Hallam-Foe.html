Hallam Foe (2007): Story, Cast & What to Know

Hallam Foe is a dark British coming-of-age drama directed by David Mackenzie. Jamie Bell plays Hallam, a troubled young man whose grief over his mother's death shapes the way he sees the people around him.

Living in Edinburgh, Hallam develops a habit of watching other people from a distance. His fascination with Kate Breck, played by Sophia Myles, becomes especially intense because she reminds him of his late mother. The resulting relationship is deliberately uneasy, blending mystery, attraction, grief and a young man's struggle to understand himself.
