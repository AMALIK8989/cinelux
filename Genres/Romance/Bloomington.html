Bloomington (2010)

About the Film

A Complicated College Romance

Bloomington (2010) is an independent coming-of-age drama written and directed by Fernanda Cardoso. Former child star Jackie Kirk moves from California to a college in Bloomington, Indiana, hoping to build a life beyond her television past. At a university gathering, she meets Catherine Stark, a professor whose confidence and reputation intrigue her. Their attraction develops into a secret affair, but the relationship becomes increasingly difficult to separate from Jackie's chance to return to acting and Catherine’s position at the university. The film explores desire, independence, identity and the consequences of a relationship shaped by an uneven balance of experience and authority.
