About the Film

An Orderly Life, and the Pull She Can't Explain Away

Camille and her fiancé Martin are "career Christians," urged by their college to marry so they can take over as co-chaplains once the current reverend retires — a future Camille has accepted without much question. But when her beloved dog dies and Camille finds herself unmoored, a laundromat encounter with Petra, an outspoken and magnetic performer in a modern, Fellini-esque circus troupe, sparks something Camille can't rationalize away. As she's pulled deeper into Petra's orbit and her own desires, Camille has to reckon with everything she thought she knew about her faith, her relationship, and herself. Written and directed by Patricia Rozema, When Night Is Falling premiered in competition at the Berlin International Film Festival and has endured as a landmark work of Canadian LGBTQ+ cinema.
