About the Film

A Marriage, A Ministry, and a Love She Didn't Expect

Elena is the devoted wife of a pastor whose sermons leave no room for the feelings she's starting to have. When she meets Peyton, a confident and openly gay writer, the connection between them is immediate and impossible to explain away. As their bond grows, Elena is forced to confront everything she's built her identity around — her marriage, her faith, and finally, herself.
