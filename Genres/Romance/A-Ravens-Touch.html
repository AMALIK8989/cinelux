About the Film

Grief, Guilt, and an Unexpected Path Back

Raven hasn't forgiven herself for the accident, and the cabin was supposed to be a place to disappear. But Kate's arrival with her two children changes the shape of Raven's isolation — first as an intrusion, then as company neither of them planned for. As the days pass, their shared vulnerability opens something neither woman expected: a way back to feeling, to trust, and maybe to each other.
