About the Film

A Love Painted in Secret

Marianne arrives on an isolated coastal estate with a covert assignment: paint Héloïse's wedding portrait without her knowledge, since Héloïse has refused every previous painter sent to capture her likeness. As the two women share their days walking the cliffs and evenings by candlelight, careful observation turns into mutual longing — a bond that can only exist within the borrowed time before the portrait is finished.
