About the Film

Identity, Imitation & Hidden Secrets

Bedspacer is a 2024 Filipino drama-thriller directed by Carlo Obispo. The story centers on Janice, whose fascination with her new roommate Lexi grows into an urge to copy Lexi’s style and persona. As Janice pursues campus attention and the two women become increasingly entangled, buried secrets begin to blur the line between authenticity and seductive deception. The film explores identity, envy, desire, social performance and the consequences of trying to become someone else.
