About the Film

A Complicated Family Dynamic

Directed by Christopher Novabos and written by King Abalos, Stepdaddy is a 2026 Filipino drama centered on sisters Chloe and Nica. Their separate romantic entanglements with Carl, their stepfather, create an emotionally charged and controversial family situation. The film builds its story around the complicated relationships within the household, examining attraction, tension, boundaries and the consequences of choices that disrupt family roles. Led by Astrid Lee, Christy Imperial, Jamilla Obispo and Jhon Mark Marcia, the movie is a mature-themed Vivamax production in Tagalog.
