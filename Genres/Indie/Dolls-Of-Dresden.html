About the Film

Love, Art, Loss & The Search for Belonging

Anna is a young, progressive journalist living in Thessaloniki. Her chance meeting with Lophilia, a painter born in Dresden whose unconventional approach to life and art both fascinates and challenges her, develops into a deep romantic relationship. As the women begin imagining a future and the possibility of building a family together, their story takes a tragic turn with Lophilia’s sudden death. Anna then travels to Dresden, determined to deal with the practical and emotional consequences of Lophilia’s wishes and her relationship with her father.

Cinelux Editorial Standards: This Dolls of Dresden film page is based on production records, festival information, Greek film listings, and established film databases. The film was directed by Alexis Tsafas and written by Alexia Beziki and Alexis Tsafas. It is a Greece-Germany co-production, runs approximately 98 minutes, and stars Christina Sotiriou and Alexia Beziki. The film premiered at the 64th Thessaloniki International Film Festival in November 2023 and received the festival's Original Music Award; it also won Best Movie at the Buzau International Arts Festival in 2024.

Frequently Asked Questions

Dolls of Dresden follows Anna, a young journalist in Thessaloniki, whose chance meeting with Lophilia, a Dresden-born painter, develops into an intense relationship. Their hopes for a future together and a family are interrupted by Lophilia’s sudden death, sending Anna to Dresden to confront the consequences of her loss.

The film was directed by Alexis Tsafas and written by Alexia Beziki and Alexis Tsafas. The leading roles are played by Christina Sotiriou as Anna and Alexia Beziki as Lophilia, with Yorgos Glastras, Sotirios Psaltidis, Helga Werner, Peter Meinhardt, Akilas Karazisis and others in supporting roles.

Cinematek lists the film with a K-13 classification and a runtime of 98 minutes. IMDb also lists the runtime as 1 hour 38 minutes.

Dolls of Dresden had its world premiere at the 64th Thessaloniki International Film Festival on November 10, 2023. The film later received the festival's Original Music Award and won Best Movie at the Buzau International Arts Festival in 2024.
