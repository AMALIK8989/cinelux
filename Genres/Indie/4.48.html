About the Film

A Role That Begins to Reshape Real Life

4.48 is a French independent drama about the psychological intensity of acting and the fragile boundary between an actor’s lived experience and a role. The story follows an ambitious performer as she prepares for a challenging audition, immersing herself so completely in the character that her relationships and choices begin to shift. Rather than a conventional backstage story, the film examines artistic ambition, identity, desire and the risks of losing perspective while pursuing a performance.
