About the Film

A Wish, a Friendship, and New Feelings

Therese dela Cruz (Robb Guinto) learns she has a life-limiting illness and decides to make the most of the time she has left. She turns to her best friend Maddie Reyes (Erika Balagtas) for help meeting men and fulfilling her wishes. As Therese searches for meaningful experiences, the friendship between the two women deepens, while Maddie’s relationship with her boyfriend Arthur (Ardy Raymundo) adds another emotional complication.

Frequently Asked Questions

Late Bloomer follows Therese, who receives a life-limiting diagnosis and asks her best friend Maddie to help her make the most of her remaining time. Their closeness leads to unexpected romantic feelings.

Late Bloomer was directed by Rodante Y. Pajemna Jr.

The principal cast includes Robb Guinto as Therese, Erika Balagtas as Maddie, Ardy Raymundo as Aries, Mikhael Padua as Arthur, and Jasper Torres as Luigi.

Late Bloomer premiered on Vivamax / VMX on 30 April 2024.
