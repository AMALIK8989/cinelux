About the Film

A Fantasy Quest Between Worlds

Dagger Kiss: Enchanted Forest (2020) follows sorceress Arden and her warrior girlfriend Katia as they navigate the divide between Earth and the World of Magic. When the dark sorcerer Zareth returns, the pair are drawn into a quest to stop him. The film blends fantasy adventure, science-fiction elements and a central romantic relationship, and is based on the Dagger Kiss series.
