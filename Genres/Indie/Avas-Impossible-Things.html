About the Film

Ava’s Dream World Blurs Love, Memory and Reality

Directed and written by Marina Rice Bader, Ava's Impossible Things is an intimate fantasy drama about a daughter whose life has narrowed around caring for her mother. Ava and Faye share a deep bond shaped by art, affection and a belief in magic, but an illness casts a long shadow over their future. When unexpected visitors and a startling revelation disrupt an otherwise familiar day, Ava struggles to process the news and slips into an imagined world populated by people from her past and emotions she has kept at a distance. The film moves between everyday family drama and a symbolic dreamscape, exploring caregiving, grief, desire, memory and the difficult path toward acceptance.

Frequently Asked Questions

The film follows Ava, who retreats into an imaginative dream world after a difficult revelation about her mother and their future. The story explores love, caregiving, grief, memory and acceptance.

Ava's Impossible Things was written and directed by Marina Rice Bader.

The principal cast includes Chloe Farnworth, Susan Duerden, Abigail Titmuss, Lauryn Nicole Hamilton and Marc Hawes.

The film premiered in 2016 and received a wider digital release in 2017.

Its listed runtime is approximately 80 minutes; some platform listings show 78 or 79 minutes.

It is a drama and romance with fantasy elements, moving between everyday family life and Ava's imagined dream world.

Chloe Farnworth plays Ava. Susan Duerden portrays Faye and Claire, while Abigail Titmuss plays Anna and Freya.

The story considers the bond between a mother and daughter, the strain of caregiving, mortality, grief, desire, imagination and the process of accepting change.
