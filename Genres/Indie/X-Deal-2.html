About the Film

An Island Getaway and a Complicated Agreement

Peter and Violet travel to a resort island for a pictorial with a client, where they unexpectedly meet Olivia, Peter’s ex. Hoping to find closure, Peter reconnects with Olivia. Violet proposes an arrangement that also acknowledges Olivia’s attraction to her, and the three agree to an exchange that soon becomes more complicated than expected. As expectations shift and Peter pushes beyond the original terms, the arrangement strains trust and exposes the fragile boundaries between desire, jealousy, and control.
