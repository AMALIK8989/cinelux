About the Film

An Unexpected Connection Amid Grief and Family Expectations

After the death of her girlfriend, Lauren, a young artist, returns to her family home in Oklahoma to find comfort and rebuild her life. Her mother sees the move as a chance for Lauren to follow a more conventional path, but Lauren is still navigating grief and her own identity. When her brother Justin arrives with his fiancée Carrie to help launch a local winery, Lauren and Carrie begin working together on its artwork and marketing. Their collaboration grows into an unexpected emotional connection, challenging the expectations surrounding both women and setting a complicated family drama in motion. Directed by Maura Anderson, the film is rooted in the landscapes and social expectations of suburban Oklahoma. Its intimate storytelling focuses on the complicated ways people seek comfort, belonging and a new sense of possibility after loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

Heartland follows Lauren, an Oklahoma artist grieving the death of her girlfriend, who returns to her family home and unexpectedly grows close to her brother’s fiancée, Carrie.

Heartland was directed by Maura Anderson. The screenplay was written by Velinda Godfrey and Todd Waring.

The film stars Velinda Godfrey as Lauren, Laura Spencer as Carrie, Beth Grant as Crystal, Steve Agee as JD and Eve Gordon as Sherri.

Heartland was released in 2017, with its streaming release listed as June 6, 2017.
