About the Film

First Love, Friendship & Finding Yourself

Written and directed by Ric Forster and produced by Melanie Rowland, My First Girl Crush explores the uncertainty of a first crush through Ingrid’s growing feelings for Stella. Ingrid must navigate the social pressure of high school alongside the expectations of her conservative Chinese-Australian family. The story centres on friendship, identity, belonging and the difficult choice of whether to reveal feelings that could change a close relationship.
