Sorority (2025)

About the Film

Inside the Secrets of an Elite Sorority

Directed and written by Sigrid Polon, Sorority (2025) is a Filipino romantic drama about Cassidy, a fearless pre-law student who enters an elite sorority led by the influential Selene. As Cassidy becomes drawn to a woman who awakens unexpected feelings, the group’s secrets place desire, loyalty and ambition on a collision course. Set within a world of privilege and social power, the film explores the complicated bonds that form behind the sorority’s polished image.
