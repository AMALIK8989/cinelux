Loving Annabelle (2006)

About the Film

A Boarding School Drama Built on One Secret

Loving Annabelle is a 2006 American drama written and directed by Katherine Brooks. Erin Kelly plays Annabelle Tillman, a teenager who has been expelled from two schools for disruptive behaviour and is sent to a Roman Catholic boarding school by her disapproving mother. There she becomes involved with her teacher, Simone Bradley, played by Diane Gaidry. The film is inspired by the 1931 German film Mädchen in Uniform, and Brooks has said that news coverage of teacher and student relationships shaped her script. Critics noted that the film sets up a difficult moral situation and leaves its questions open for the viewer. The school scenes were shot at Marymount High School in Los Angeles. The film won the Audience Award at the 2006 Fort Worth Gay and Lesbian International Film Festival and was an Outfest award winner that year.

Annabelle, a student who has been expelled from two schools, is sent to a Roman Catholic boarding school by her mother. She falls for her teacher, Simone Bradley, and the two begin a relationship that they must keep secret.

Loving Annabelle was written and directed by Katherine Brooks. The film is inspired by the 1931 German film Mädchen in Uniform.

The film stars Erin Kelly as Annabelle Tillman and Diane Gaidry as Simone Bradley. Laura Breckenridge, Michelle Horn, Gustine Fudickar and Ilene Graff also appear in the cast.

Loving Annabelle was released in 2006. Wolfe Video released the Region 1 DVD on December 12, 2006.
