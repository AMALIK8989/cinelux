About the Film

A Chance Meeting, a Forbidden Affair, a City That Can't Contain It

Maya spends her days chasing stories for a small Athens news outlet, restless and searching for something that matters. Samar arrived in Greece after fleeing Afghanistan and the Taliban, carrying a past she's still learning how to speak about. When the two women meet, an instant, electric connection pulls them into a relationship neither of them planned for — one built on stolen moments, mutual risk, and a hunger to be truly seen. But as their affair deepens, it starts to expose them both: Samar's safety becomes precarious in ways Maya never anticipated, while Maya's account of their romance begins spreading online, turning something private into something the whole city is suddenly watching. Caught between desire and consequence, Maya and Samar must decide what they're willing to sacrifice for each other.
