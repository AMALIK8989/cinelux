About the Film

A Coming-of-Age Romance at the Panagbenga Festival

Directed by Rainerio Yamson II and released on VMX on April 15, 2025, Violet is a Philippine drama about a young woman discovering herself. Set against the bright celebrations of the Panagbenga Festival, the film follows Violet, played by Aliya Raymundo, as she navigates first love, desire and the awakening of her sexuality. Christy Imperial, Dani Yoshida and Ralph Engle round out the cast. The film is made for adult audiences: it is a restricted title that contains nudity and mature themes, so viewers should check the content information for their region before watching.
