Maharot (2024)

About the Film

A 2024 Vivamax Drama About Love and Life’s Twists

Maharot is a 2024 Filipino drama from Vivamax, directed by Rodante Pajemna Jr. and released with a world premiere on July 19, 2024. The story follows Tantan, a young man from a small town, who falls for Joni, a free-spirited woman who sells herself yet teaches him about love. As their bond deepens, the film asks whether their love can survive life’s twists. The cast includes Aiko Garcia as Joni, VR Relosa as Tantan, Athena Red as Helen, Ardy Raymundo as Tasyo and Cherrylyn David as Tacing. At 46 minutes, the film is rated R-18 and contains adult content, so it is intended for adult viewers only.
