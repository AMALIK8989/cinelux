About the Film

Habal (2025): A Filipino Drama of Desire, Discovery & Deception

Habal is a 2025 Filipino drama-romance directed by Bobby Bonifacio Jr. The story follows Danilo (JD Aguas), a reserved motorcycle taxi driver whose roommates tease him for his lack of romantic and sexual experience. His circumstances begin to change after he meets Erika (Athena Red), a passenger whose confidence draws him into a new world, and her best friend Lara (Karen Lopez).

As Danilo spends more time with the two women, curiosity turns into a series of unexpected encounters. But the relationships are not as straightforward as they first appear: secrets surrounding Erika introduce deception and consequences, forcing Danilo to navigate desire, trust and the choices that follow. The film also features Marlon Marcia, Queenie De Mesa, Basti Flores, Ivan Ponce and Jodi Garcia.
