About the Film

A New Crush, Changing Friendships & Growing Up

Written and directed by Ric Forster and produced by Melanie Rowland, My New Girl Crush follows Ingrid as she works on a school project with Brayden and becomes increasingly drawn to his older sister Dani. The film explores attraction, friendship and the awkward emotional decisions that come with growing up.
