Private Tutor (2024): Story, Cast & What to Know Before Watching

Private Tutor is a 2024 Filipino drama directed by Ryan Evangelista. The story follows Joy, a struggling teacher who takes on private tutoring work with Erica. What begins as a job becomes increasingly complicated when Erica's wealthy widowed father, Gordon, develops feelings for Joy while Joy is already dealing with an unhappy relationship with Vigo.

The film uses the tutoring arrangement as the starting point for a story about attraction, relationship pressure and difficult personal choices. With Zsara Laxamana in the lead role of Joy and Christy Imperial as Erica, the movie places its characters inside a tense domestic and romantic situation where private boundaries become increasingly difficult to keep separate from everyday life.
