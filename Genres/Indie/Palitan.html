Palitan (2021)

About the Film

Old Love, New Tensions and a Complicated Reunion

Directed by Brillante Mendoza and written by Honeylyn Joy Alipio, Palitan is a Filipino drama about relationships that refuse to stay in the past. Jen, an openly bisexual woman, travels to her hometown with her partner James, whose struggles have placed strain on their relationship. There she encounters Marie, her former best friend and first love, who is now engaged to Al, a mutual friend. What begins as a homecoming becomes a charged reunion between two couples, as attraction, memory and unresolved emotions complicate the bonds between them. The film explores intimacy, jealousy, desire and the fragile boundaries within relationships.
