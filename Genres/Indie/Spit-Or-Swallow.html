About the Film

Desire, Validation & Self-Discovery

Lena (Kim Yashii) is a quiet college student whose understanding of relationships has mostly come from books. Her world shifts when she meets Cole (Ghion Espinosa), a confident playboy who gives her the attention she has been seeking. At the same time, Lena feels drawn to Astra Polastro (Rinoa Halili), a substitute professor whose presence awakens a different kind of curiosity. As attraction, expectations and manipulation become tangled, Lena must confront what she really wants and how much of herself she is willing to give up to feel accepted.
