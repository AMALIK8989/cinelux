Professor Marston and the Wonder Women (2017)

About the Film

The Unconventional Love Story Behind an Icon

Harvard psychologist William Moulton Marston and his brilliant, sharp-tongued wife Elizabeth are collaborators in both marriage and research when they hire a young student, Olive Byrne, as their teaching assistant. As the three grow closer, they enter a relationship that challenges the expectations of the world around them. Their shared research and ideas about love, female dominance, and the lie detector ultimately help inspire the creation of Wonder Woman.
