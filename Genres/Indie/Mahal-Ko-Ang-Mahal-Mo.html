About the Film

A Vivamax Drama From Direk Aya

Mahal Ko Ang Mahal Mo is a 2024 Filipino drama released as a Vivamax production. Direk Aya directed the film, which marked the first time the director worked on a Vivamax project. The cast is led by Angelica Hart, Angeline Aril and Van Allen Ong, with Mia Cruz and Gab Deiparine in supporting roles. Trade coverage described the film as a sexy drama, and reviewers noted that it includes intimate scenes. The film runs about 48 minutes, according to the listing I found.
