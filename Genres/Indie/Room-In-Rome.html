Room in Rome (2010)

About the Film

One Night in Rome

Written and directed by Julio Medem, Room in Rome follows Alba, a thirty-something Spanish tourist, and Natasha, a younger Russian woman, through a single night in a hotel room in the Italian capital. The film was shot in Rome and follows the story over the course of the night, moving from a chance meeting to an intimate and emotional connection. Medem makes the film in English, Spanish and Russian, and it became his first English-language feature. Elena Anaya plays Alba and Natasha Yarovenko plays Natasha, with Enrico Lo Verso and Najwa Nimri in supporting roles. The story explores desire, loneliness and the feeling of stepping outside ordinary life for a few hours. Its tone is intimate and often melancholic, and the film has been noted for the performances of its two leads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Alba, a Spanish tourist in her thirties, meets a younger Russian woman named Natasha on the last night of their holidays in Rome. They spend the night together in Alba’s hotel room, and the encounter leaves a lasting mark on both of them.

Room in Rome was directed by Julio Medem, who also co-wrote the screenplay with Julio Rojas and Katherine Fugate.

The film stars Elena Anaya as Alba and Natasha Yarovenko as Natasha. Enrico Lo Verso and Najwa Nimri also appear in the cast.

Room in Rome was released in Spain in May 2010. It was released in the United States later that year under the English title Room in Rome.

Room in Rome has a runtime of approximately 107 minutes (1 hour 47 minutes).

Room in Rome is not rated by the MPAA. It contains strong adult content, so check the content information for your region before watching.
