About the Film

A Dangerous Discovery in the Mountains

A photographer sent into the mountains to capture a rare flower discovers women trapped in a hidden brothel serving illegal loggers. He risks everything to help them escape. Directed by Brillante Mendoza, the film blends drama and suspense against the backdrop of remote forest communities and illegal logging.
