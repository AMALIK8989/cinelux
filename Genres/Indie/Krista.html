Krista (2024)

About the Film

A Wife’s Loyalty Put to the Ultimate Test

Directed by Sidney Zaid Pascua and written by Quinn Carrillo, Krista is a Filipino crime and romantic drama about the fragile boundary between love and desperation. Krista and her husband Makoy begin with an ordinary life, but his costly gambling loss leads him into a criminal situation that threatens to unravel everything they have built. As the consequences close in, Krista is pushed toward a painful decision in an attempt to save him. The film explores devotion, sacrifice, financial pressure and the difficult choices people make when the person they love is in danger.
