The Light Touch (2020)

About the Film

A Playful Athens Romance Turns into a Pickpocket Chase

Directed by Robert MacLean, The Light Touch is a light-footed romantic comedy set against the streets and holiday atmosphere of Athens. Becky is a clever, mischievous pickpocket who notices Miranda while working a crowded metro carriage. The attraction is immediate, and the pair’s budding connection sends the story into romantic-comedy territory. But Becky’s nimble fingers get her into trouble when she accidentally lifts the wallet of a policeman on vacation. He becomes fixated on catching her, turning the women’s getaway into an increasingly amusing game of pursuit. With its Greek setting, queer love story and caper-style premise, the film blends romance, comedy and an indie sensibility.
