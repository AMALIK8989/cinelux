About the Film

A Reunion That Complicates Love and Family

Directed by Ray-An Ludwig Peralta and written by Quinn Carrillo, Hiram centers on Grace and Ivy, half-sisters who reunite in New Zealand and attempt to begin a shared chapter of their lives. Their reunion brings more than a renewed family connection: the two women become entangled in an emotional situation involving Felix, creating a delicate tension between affection, loyalty and personal desire. Set against the experience of starting over far from home, the film frames its romantic conflict through the complicated bond between the sisters. Hiram is a Filipino drama-romance produced with Vivamax, 3:16 Media Network and Vipe Studios.
