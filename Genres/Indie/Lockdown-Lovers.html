Lockdown Lovers (2023)

About the Film

A Lockdown Romance Set in Melbourne

Lockdown Lovers is a 2023 Australian romance directed by Ric Forster from a screenplay by Danny Spring. Kate Mayhew plays Kristal, a visitor enjoying the final days of her trip, who meets Lisette, played by Stevie McKeon, on a night out in Melbourne. The story begins in the early days of the COVID-19 pandemic, when the two women are suddenly stuck together for a month. Their relationship is tested by their differences as much as it is pulled together by the strange circumstances they share. Forster also shot and edited the film. Lockdown Lovers was produced by Lilydale Films, the company behind the online series Flunk, and it was released on Vimeo OnDemand before it was set to stream on Tubi. Its portrait of a city in lockdown and its focus on intimate, everyday connection are central to its appeal.

What happens in the story

Kristal and Lisette have only just met when Melbourne shuts down. Lin Yin plays Freya and Hannah Brooke plays Alison, and the film follows how the two leads adjust to living together under lockdown rules.

Frequently Asked Questions

Set in Melbourne during the early days of the COVID-19 pandemic, Lockdown Lovers follows Kristal, who meets Lisette on a night out during the last days of her trip to Australia. Stuck together during the lockdown, the two must find out whether their differences will drive them apart or bring them closer.

Lockdown Lovers was directed by Ric Forster, who also served as the film’s cinematographer and editor. Danny Spring wrote the screenplay.

The film stars Kate Mayhew as Kristal and Stevie McKeon as Lisette. Lin Yin plays Freya and Hannah Brooke plays Alison.

Lockdown Lovers was released on July 31, 2023. It was produced by Lilydale Films, the Australian company behind the online series Flunk, and it became available on Vimeo OnDemand before streaming on Tubi.
