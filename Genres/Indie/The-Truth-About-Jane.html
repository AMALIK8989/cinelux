The Truth About Jane (2000)

About the Film

A Coming-Out Story Told Through a Mother and Daughter

The Truth About Jane is a 2000 American drama written and directed by Lee Rose and first broadcast on Lifetime. Ellen Muth plays Jane, a high school student who discovers that her feelings for a new girl in her class are sexual in nature. Stockard Channing plays her mother, Janice, who struggles to accept the revelation. Jane turns for support to Jimmy, a gay friend of her mother, played by RuPaul in a rare dramatic role. The film follows the family’s conflict and its slow path toward acceptance. Its ending includes a dedication to Matthew Shepard and to all the men and women who love differently. The film was released on August 7, 2000, and runs about 91 minutes, according to the listings I found. Terence Blanchard composed the score.
