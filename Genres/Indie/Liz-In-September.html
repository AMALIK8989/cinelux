Liz in September (2014)

About the Film

Healing, Friendship and Second Chances

Directed by Venezuelan filmmaker Fina Torres, Liz in September is based on Jane Chambers’ groundbreaking play Last Summer at Bluefish Cove, which Torres adapted for the screen. Patricia Velásquez plays Liz, a successful model whose life is upended by a serious illness. Her path crosses with a woman who has lost her son to cancer, and the two form a connection built on grief, honesty and sisterhood. The film was shot in Spanish and premiered in Caracas in October 2014 before travelling to international LGBTQ film festivals. Its story is about the emotional connections that go beyond sexuality, and it earned the Audience Award for Feature Film at the Miami Gay and Lesbian Film Festival in 2015. Critics noted the film’s sincere portrayal of friendship, even as they found its emotional tone at times heavy-handed.

Liz, a successful model facing a terminal illness, bonds with a woman who lost her son to cancer. Their friendship brings healing and makes both of them reconsider their values and priorities.

Liz in September was directed by Fina Torres, who also co-wrote the screenplay and produced the film with Judy Miller and Laura Oramas.

The film stars Patricia Velásquez as Liz, with Eloísa Maturén as Eva, Mimí Lazo as Dolores, Elba Escobar as Margot, Danay García as Coqui, Arlette Torres as Any, Sheila Monterola as Eulalia, María Luisa Flores as Alex and Luis Gerónimo Abreu as Carlos.

Yes. The screenplay is an adaptation of Jane Chambers’ play Last Summer at Bluefish Cove, which Torres adapted for the screen with Chambers credited as a writer.
