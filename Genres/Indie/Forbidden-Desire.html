About the Film

A Relationship Tested by Secrets

Forbidden Desire (2024) is a Filipino drama centered on Agatha and Ubel, former lovers whose complicated connection refuses to end after their breakup. Agatha tries to move forward with a new partner, but her continuing affair with Ubel leads to a revelation with potentially devastating consequences. Directed by G.B. Sampedro, the film explores secrecy, desire, trust and the fallout of a relationship that remains unresolved.
