Sila ay Akin (2023)

About the Film

A Family Drama Under One Roof

Sila ay Akin is a 2023 Filipino drama directed by Mac Alejandre, with a story by National Artist Ricky Lee. The film follows a household of six people whose lives are pulled apart by poverty, hardship and the pressures of city life. Angeli Khang plays Josie, the wife of Pau, played by Vince Rillon, and Azi Acosta plays Maritess, who is paired with Victor Relosa as JC. Cess Garcia plays Queenie, and Angie Castrence plays Celing. The women of the household remain at the center of the story, trying to hold the family together as the problems mount. The film was released on Vivamax on October 27, 2023, and runs about 104 minutes. Because the film deals with heavy and mature themes, viewer discretion is advised.
