About the Film

69+1 (2021): An Unconventional Take on the Seven-Year Itch

69+1 is a 2021 Filipino comedy directed and written by Darryl Yap. The story follows a lesbian couple whose relationship has reached the familiar seven-year itch. Hoping to change the dynamic, they turn to their male friend and ask him to become part of their relationship. What begins as an unusual solution opens the door to complications around intimacy, trust, boundaries and expectations.

Starring Janno Gibbs, Maui Taylor and Rose Van Ginkel, the film blends adult relationship themes with comedy. This CineLux guide brings together the film’s cast, synopsis, release information and viewing details for audiences searching for 69+1, 69 Plus 1 or the 2021 Filipino comedy.
