Rapsa (2025)

About the Film

A VMX Drama About Boundaries at Work

Rapsa is a 2025 Filipino drama, romance and suspense film released exclusively on VMX on May 2, 2025. Directed by Topel Lee and written by Frederick Castro, it follows a manager who is drawn to her naive employee. Their attraction grows into an entanglement that blurs professional boundaries and leaves both of them emotionally changed. Micaella Raz plays Ella, JD Aguas plays Dino and Vern Kaye plays Ariel, with Shiena Yu as Rita and Alona Navarro as Miko. Helen Villamar and Steven Kho produced the film. The film contains adult content and is intended for adult viewers only. If you also enjoy Vern Kaye’s work, see Trianggulo, or try Pihit for another VMX-style drama.
