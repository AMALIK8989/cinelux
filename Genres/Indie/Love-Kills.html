About the Film

A Romance That Turns Dangerous

Love Kills is a 2024 drama directed by Duke, released on video on demand and digital platforms on June 4, 2024. Gaia Brooks plays Lia, and Lucy Diamante plays Brianna, the film’s two leads. Konstantinos Angel plays Cashmeer, and Maximilian Seed plays Milo. The story centres on an affair that comes to an abrupt end once the lovers are caught. Reviews have focused on the chemistry between the two leads, with one critic writing that the film relies heavily on sex and violence to keep audiences engaged. The film runs about 100 minutes and is listed as Not Rated.
