About the Film

Freedom, Desire & Dangerous Consequences

Directed by Roman Perez Jr., Adan follows Ellen, a provincial young woman desperate to gain independence from her abusive father. Her plan pulls her best friend Marian into a dangerous chain of events, blending desire, lies, crime and psychological tension.
