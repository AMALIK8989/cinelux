About the Film

A Complicated Connection Behind the Screen

Directed by Iar Arondaing and written by John Carlo Pacala, Kaulayaw centers on Ella and Cara, two college schoolmates who enter the world of online sexual entertainment to meet their financial needs. What begins as a practical arrangement gradually becomes emotionally complicated as the pair spend more time together and discover an attraction that goes beyond their work. The 2024 Filipino drama explores intimacy, economic pressure, identity and the blurred boundaries between a performed online persona and genuine connection.
