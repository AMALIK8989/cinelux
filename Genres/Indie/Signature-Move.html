Signature Move (2017)

About the Film

A Romantic Comedy About Family, Faith and Wrestling

Signature Move is a 2017 American comedy-drama directed by Jennifer Reeder. Fawzia Mirza wrote the film with Lisa Donato, produced it, and stars as Zaynab, a Pakistani-American immigration lawyer in Chicago who is also a Muslim lesbian. Zaynab cares for her TV-obsessed widowed mother, Parveen, played by Shabana Azmi. When she begins a romance with Alma, played by Sari Sanchez, a confident Mexican-American woman whose mother is a former professional luchadora, Zaynab takes up wrestling herself. The film premiered at the 2017 South by Southwest Film Festival and then played widely on the LGBTQ+ festival circuit. It won an audience award for Best Actress for Mirza at FilmOut San Diego. The film runs about 80 minutes, and it is listed in English, Spanish and Hindi, with English subtitles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Zaynab, a Pakistani-American lawyer in Chicago, cares for her widowed mother Parveen and starts a romance with Alma, a Mexican-American woman whose mother is a former professional luchadora. Zaynab takes up wrestling as she searches for her identity in life, love and wrestling.

Signature Move was directed by Jennifer Reeder. Fawzia Mirza and Lisa Donato wrote the screenplay, and Mirza also stars in the film as Zaynab.

The film stars Fawzia Mirza as Zaynab and Shabana Azmi as Parveen. Sari Sanchez plays Alma, and Audrey Francis, Charin Alvarez, Mark Hood and Molly Brennan also appear in the cast.

Signature Move had its world premiere at the 2017 South by Southwest Film Festival. It was released in the United States on October 13, 2017, and became available to stream in February 2018.

Signature Move runs about 80 minutes (1 hour 20 minutes). Some listings show 79 minutes.
