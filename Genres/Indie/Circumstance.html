Circumstance (2011)

About the Film

Forbidden Love and Family Pressure in Tehran

Written and directed by Maryam Keshavarz, Circumstance follows Atafeh and her close friend Shireen, two teenagers who spend their nights at hidden parties in Tehran, away from the watchful eyes of their families and the state. As their bond deepens into romance, the film shows the risks they take and the small freedoms they claim. The story turns darker when Atafeh’s brother, freshly released from prison, becomes a religious zealot and begins to monitor his sister and her friend. Keshavarz’s first narrative feature premiered at the 2011 Sundance Film Festival, where it won the Audience Award for Dramatic Film. The film explores love, faith and control in contemporary Iranian youth culture, and it was banned from Iran.

Circumstance was written and directed by Maryam Keshavarz. It is her first narrative feature film.
