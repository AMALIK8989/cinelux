About the Film

A Road Trip About Healing and Connection

Superbloom is a 2025 American drama written and directed by Alexandra Swarens, who also plays Julia. One of the two women is confronting her past, and the other is searching for purpose, and their road trip becomes a journey of healing and connection. Jahnavi Alyssa plays Lena, and Marla Lizbeth Perez plays Maria. The film was produced by Violet Wave Productions and released in April 2025, with a runtime of about 86 minutes. Listings describe it as a drama, and I did not find festival awards or major critical honors for the film in the sources checked.
