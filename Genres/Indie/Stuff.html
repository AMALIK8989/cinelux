Stuff (2015)

About the Film

A Family Drama About Grief, Desire and Staying Together

Written and directed by Suzanne Guacci, Stuff follows a married lesbian couple raising two daughters. Deb Murdoch, played by Yvonne Jung, and Trish Murdoch, played by Karen Sillas, are in the middle of a midlife crisis, and grief and new attraction put their domestic life under strain. Traci Dinwiddie plays Jamie, and the cast also includes Phyllis Somerville, Kevin Brown, Maya Guacci, Brianna Scudiero, Vincent Colon and Joseph Halsey. The film was produced by Theresa Aquilina, Suzanne Guacci and Debra Markowitz, and it received awards in 2016 for its direction and performances. It is rated TV-MA and deals with mature themes, so it is intended for adult viewers.
