About the Film

A Female-Led Neo-Noir About Healing

Serpentine Pink is a 2022 American independent film directed by Vivian Sorenson, with a screenplay by Megan Breen. Its story is set in Joshua Tree, California, and it is built around a group of eclectic characters, led by Sabina Zúñiga Varela as Jaymee and Kristin Condon as Henrietta. The film is described as a surreal, female-centric work that examines the different ways a heart heals from trauma. Hard Knock Productions and 1157 Productions produced the film, which runs about 82 minutes. Ron Hanks produced the film, with Larissa D’Andrea as executive producer. Its first release was on September 2, 2022, and it reached video on demand and digital platforms on June 10, 2025.
