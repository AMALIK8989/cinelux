About the Film

A DJ, New Attraction and Different Ideas of Love

Directed by Aya Topacio and written by Quinn Carrillo, Baligtaran is a Filipino drama-romance about attraction, honesty and the expectations people bring into relationships. Kat is a beautiful DJ who catches the attention of Denise, a pansexual woman who is comfortable having multiple partners. As Kat falls for Denise, their different views on exclusivity become central to the relationship. The film explores whether Kat can accept Denise’s relationship arrangement or whether Denise might reconsider her own approach for Kat. Also known by the alternate title Baliskat, the film features Apple Dy, Skye Gonzaga and Calvin Reyes.
