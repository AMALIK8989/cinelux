About the Film

Desire, Ambition and Consequences

When Jenny enters the lives of people living in a quiet boarding house, her presence begins to change the atmosphere around them. Abel, Ephraim and Euka are drawn into a strange series of encounters, while the stories Jenny tells leave them questioning who she really is. Putahe blends mystery, drama and psychological tension around a newcomer who may not be what she first appears to be.
