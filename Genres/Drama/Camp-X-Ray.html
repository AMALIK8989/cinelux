Camp X-Ray (2014)

About the Film

A Friendship Forged Inside Guantánamo Bay

Camp X-Ray is a 2014 American drama written and directed by Peter Sattler in his directorial debut. Set at the Guantánamo Bay detention facility, it follows Army private Amy Cole, played by Kristen Stewart, who is assigned to monitor detainees. She forms an unlikely friendship with Ali, played by Payman Maadi, a prisoner who has been held there for eight years. John Carroll Lynch plays Colonel James Drummond, and Lane Garrison also appears. The film premiered at the Sundance Film Festival on January 17, 2014, and was released in US cinemas by IFC Films on October 17, 2014. It runs 117 minutes and is rated R for language and brief nude images. Critics praised the performances of Stewart and Maadi. For another drama about a difficult real-world subject, try Cocaine Godmother or Chloé.
