Like Sunday, Like Rain (2014)

About the Film

An Unlikely Friendship Between a Musician and a Cello Prodigy

Like Sunday, Like Rain is a 2014 American drama written and directed by Frank Whaley, his fourth feature film. Leighton Meester plays Eleanor Logan, a struggling musician who loses her waitress job and takes a live-in nanny position with Reggie, a 12-year-old cello prodigy played by Julian Shatkin in his film debut. Reggie’s wealthy mother, Barbara Kipper, is played by Debra Messing, and Billie Joe Armstrong plays Dennis, Eleanor’s former boyfriend. The supporting cast includes Olga Merediz, Sammy Pignalosa, Georgia Ximenes Lifsher, James McCaffrey and Olivia Luccardi, among others listed below. Monterey Media distributed the film, which opened in limited US theaters in March 2015. It runs 104 minutes and is rated R for language. It won six awards at the 2014 Williamsburg Independent Film Festival, including Best Film and Best Director. For another drama with a music or coming-of-age thread, see Mosquita y Mari or The Fallout.
