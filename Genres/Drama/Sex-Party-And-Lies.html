Sex, Party & Lies (2009)

About the Film

A Spanish Youth Ensemble Comedy-Drama

Sex, Party & Lies (Spanish title: Mentiras y gordas) is a 2009 Spanish coming-of-age comedy-drama directed by Alfonso Albacete and David Menkes. The screenplay is credited to Albacete, Ángeles González-Sinde and Menkes, and Gerardo Herrero produced. The film follows a group of young people who experiment with drugs, clubs and sex while preparing for what will be the summer of their lives, full of secrets and lies. The cast includes Mario Casas as Tony, Ana de Armas as Carola, Yon González as Nico, Ana Polvorosa as Marina, Maxi Iglesias as Pablo, Marieta Orozco as Sonia, Alejo Sauras as Bubu and Hugo Silva as Carlos, along with Asier Etxeandia, Duna Jové, Esmeralda Moya, Miriam Giovanelli, Aída Folch, Marilyn Torres and Xacobe Sanz. The film opened in Spain on March 27, 2009, and runs 107 minutes. MUBI tags it as a drama with LGBTQ+ elements. It deals with drug use and sexual content, so it is recommended for adult viewers. For another LGBTQ+ drama, see Disobedience or Mosquita y Mari.

A group of young people is preparing for what will be the summer of their lives, full of secrets, lies, relationships and nights out. The film looks at their experiments with drugs, clubs and sex.

The film was co-directed by Alfonso Albacete and David Menkes. The screenplay is credited to Albacete, Ángeles González-Sinde and Menkes, and Gerardo Herrero produced it.

Mario Casas plays Tony, Ana de Armas plays Carola, Yon González plays Nico, Ana Polvorosa plays Marina, Maxi Iglesias plays Pablo, Marieta Orozco plays Sonia, Alejo Sauras plays Bubu and Hugo Silva plays Carlos. The film opened in Spain on March 27, 2009. The Spanish title is Mentiras y gordas.

The film runs 107 minutes, or 1 hour and 47 minutes.

MUBI classifies it as a drama with LGBTQ+ elements. It is a youth ensemble film and is not the same as the UK film More Sex, Lies and Depravity.
