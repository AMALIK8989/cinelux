Love (2015)

About the Film

Gaspar Noé’s Nonlinear Romantic Drama

Love is a 2015 French-Belgian-Brazilian romantic drama written and directed by Gaspar Noé, who also produced the film. It follows Murphy, an American film student living in Paris and played by Karl Glusman. His relationship with Electra, played by Aomi Muyock, is intense and emotionally charged, and his life also includes his Danish partner Omi, played by Klara Kristin, and their young son. The story is told out of order, moving between memory and desire, and Noé uses a stylized visual approach throughout. Juan Saavedra, Ugo Fox and Vincent Maraval also appear. The film opened in limited US release on October 30, 2015, and runs 135 minutes. It is rated NC-17 in the United States and 18 by the BBFC for strong real sex. Noé has said some of the sex scenes are real and others are simulated, so this title is intended for adult viewers only. For another adult-rated romantic drama, see Fifty Shades of Grey or Blue Is the Warmest Color.
