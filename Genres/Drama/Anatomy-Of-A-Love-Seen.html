Anatomy of a Love Seen (2014)

About the Film

A Movie Within a Movie About Love on Set and Off

Anatomy of a Love Seen is a 2014 American romantic drama written and directed by Marina Rice Bader, who also appears in the cast. Zoe, a rising actress, and Mal, her co-star, fall for each other while filming a love scene, and what begins as a whirlwind affair ends in a painful breakup. The story then follows the two women as the film they made together requires them to reunite and reshoot the scene. Sharon Hinnendael plays Zoe and Jill Evyn plays Mal, with Constance Brenneman, Kieran Valla and Justin Kane in supporting roles. Justin Kane also served as cinematographer, and the music was composed by Thom Robson. Levi Smock and Jan Miller Corran produced. The film was released in the United States in July 2014, and Gravitas Ventures later acquired its distribution rights. It runs 80 minutes and is not rated. Critics who reviewed the film praised its composition and emotional honesty, though some found its subplots distracting. For another LGBTQ-themed romance, try My Days of Mercy or Lost and Delirious.
