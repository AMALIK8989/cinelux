Fifty Shades Freed (2018)

About the Film

The Final Film in the Fifty Shades Trilogy

Fifty Shades Freed is a 2018 American erotic romantic drama directed by James Foley, with a screenplay by Niall Leonard based on E. L. James’s 2012 novel. It completes the trilogy that began with Fifty Shades of Grey (2015) and continued in Fifty Shades Darker (2017). Dakota Johnson returns as Anastasia Steele and Jamie Dornan as Christian Grey, who marry and settle into a new stability. Their happy ending is threatened when Anastasia’s former boss, Jack Hyde, played by Eric Johnson, begins to stalk and threaten them. Eloise Mumford, Rita Ora, Luke Grimes, Arielle Kebbel and Tyler Hoechlin also star. The film was produced by Dana Brunetti, E. L. James, Michael De Luca and Marcus Viscidi, and Universal Pictures released it in US theaters on February 9, 2018. It runs 105 minutes and is rated R for strong sexual content, nudity and language, so it is intended for adult viewers only. To start from the beginning, see Fifty Shades of Grey.
