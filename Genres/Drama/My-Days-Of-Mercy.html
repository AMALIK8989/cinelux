My Days of Mercy (2017)

About the Film

An Unlikely Love Story Across the Death Penalty Debate

My Days of Mercy is a 2017 romantic drama directed by Tali Shalom Ezer from a screenplay by Joe Barton. Elliot Page, credited on the film as Ellen Page, plays Lucy Moro, the daughter of a man on death row. Kate Mara plays Mercy Bromage, a woman on the opposing side of the death penalty debate. Their relationship is complicated from the start, and the film follows them as they try to find their way to each other. Amy Seimetz, Brian Geraghty and Elias Koteas co-star. The film screened at the 2017 Toronto International Film Festival and was shown at the BFI Flare London LGBTQIA+ Film Festival in March 2018. It was released in the United States on July 5, 2019. It runs 108 minutes and is rated R. Critics praised the chemistry between Page and Mara, with one review calling the romance grounded even when the story leans toward melodrama. For another LGBTQ-themed drama about first love, try Lost and Delirious or Blue Is the Warmest Color.
