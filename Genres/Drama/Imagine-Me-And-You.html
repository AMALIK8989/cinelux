Imagine Me & You (2005)

About the Film

A Wedding-Day Romance About Identity and Choice

Imagine Me & You is a 2005 British-German romantic comedy-drama written and directed by Ol Parker, in his feature directing debut. Rachel, played by Piper Perabo, is a bride about to marry Heck, played by Matthew Goode. During the ceremony she notices Luce, played by Lena Headey, a florist in the congregation, and feels instantly drawn to her. As Rachel questions her sexual orientation, she must decide between her stable relationship and a new romance that feels exhilarating and unfamiliar. The supporting cast includes Celia Imrie, Anthony Head, Darren Boyd and Sue Johnston. The film was produced by Sophie Balhetchet, Barnaby Thompson and Andro Steinborn, with music by Alex Heffes. It premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival in 2005 and runs 94 minutes. Critics and LGBTQ+ outlets praised its warmth and its treatment of the central relationship. It is rated R in the United States for some language and sexual material. For a similar romance, see Ammonite or Blue Is the Warmest Color.
