Fifty Shades of Grey (2015)

About the Film

The First Film in the Fifty Shades Trilogy

Fifty Shades of Grey is a 2015 American erotic romantic drama directed by Sam Taylor-Johnson, with a screenplay by Kelly Marcel based on E. L. James’s 2011 novel. It opened on February 13, 2015, and runs 125 minutes. Dakota Johnson plays Anastasia Steele, a college senior who steps in for a sick roommate to interview businessman Christian Grey, played by Jamie Dornan. Their relationship grows into a sexual affair that tests boundaries. Jennifer Ehle, Marcia Gay Harden, Eloise Mumford, Victor Rasuk and Luke Grimes also appear. The film is rated R in the United States and contains sexual content, so it is intended for adult viewers only. It was followed by Fifty Shades Darker and Fifty Shades Freed. For another romantic drama, try Blue Is the Warmest Color or Hot Milk.
