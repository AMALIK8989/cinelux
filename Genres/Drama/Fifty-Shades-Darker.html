Fifty Shades Darker (2017)

About the Film

The Second Chapter of a Rekindled Romance

Fifty Shades Darker is a 2017 American romantic drama directed by James Foley and written by Niall Leonard. It is based on E. L. James’s 2012 novel of the same name and serves as the sequel to Fifty Shades of Grey, released in 2015. Dakota Johnson returns as Anastasia Steele and Jamie Dornan returns as Christian Grey. The story follows the couple as Christian wrestles with his inner demons while Anastasia faces the anger and envy of the women who came before her. The film premiered in Hamburg, Germany, on February 7, 2017, and was released in the United States by Universal Pictures on February 10, 2017. It runs 118 minutes and is rated R. Danny Elfman, who scored the first film, returned to compose the music. Reviews were largely negative, though the film went on to earn more than $380 million worldwide against its $55 million budget. For another drama with a difficult real-world subject, try Cocaine Godmother or Chloé.
