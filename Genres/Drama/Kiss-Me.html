Kiss Me (2011)

About the Film

A Swedish Romance About Family, Desire and Choice

Kiss Me (Swedish: Kyss mig) is a 2011 Swedish drama written and directed by Alexandra-Therese Keining. Mia is engaged to marry a man, but when she meets Frida at their parents’ engagement party, she finds herself falling for her. Frida is the daughter of Mia’s new stepmother, so the relationship tests both families and forces Mia to question the life she has planned. The cast includes Ruth Vega Fernandez, Liv Mjönes, Lena Endre, Krister Henriksson and Joakim Nätterqvist. The film was produced by Josefine Tengblad for LeBox Produktion. It runs 105 minutes and is in Swedish. It was shown at Frameline, the San Francisco LGBTQ film festival, and it earned a Guldbagge nomination for its supporting actress. It deals with adult romance and family conflict, so viewers should check content guidance before watching. For a similar LGBTQ romance, see Imagine Me & You or Ammonite.
