The Maid (2014)

About the Film

An English-Language Drama About a Teenager and His Father’s Maid

The Maid (2014) is an English-language drama written and directed by Paul Emmanuel. Jack, an American teenager, travels to England to visit his estranged father. There he forms an emotional, passionate relationship with Maria, the older foreign maid who works in his father’s house. The story follows how that relationship affects Jack and the household around him. Claire Kahane plays Maria, Ryan Cerenko plays Jack, and Olivia Jewson plays Becky. Emmanuel co-produced the film with Ronnie Gallon, and it was made by 721 Productions. The film was released on April 12, 2014, and runs 99 minutes. It deals with a romantic relationship between a teenager and an adult, so viewers should check content guidance before watching. The 2009 Chilean film La Nana, released in English as The Maid, is a different movie. For a similar drama, see My Fault or Ammonite.
