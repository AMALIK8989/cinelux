Your Fault (2024)

About the Film

The Sequel to My Fault

Your Fault (Culpa tuya) is a 2024 Spanish romantic drama directed by Domingo González, who co-wrote the screenplay with Sofía Cuenca from Mercedes Ron’s novel. It is the second book in the Culpables trilogy and follows My Fault (2023). Despite their parents’ efforts to separate them, Noah, played by Nicole Wallace, and Nick, played by Gabriel Guevara, have a love that seems unbreakable. Work and college then open their lives to new relationships. Marta Hazas plays Rafaella, Iván Sánchez plays William, Goya Toledo plays Anabel, and Gabriela Andrada plays Sofía. The film premiered in Madrid on December 18, 2024, and arrived on Prime Video on December 27, 2024. It runs 118 minutes. It is not rated in the United States and contains romantic and sexual themes and a stepsibling storyline. The UK classifier notes suicide and self-harm references, so viewers should check content guidance before watching. To start from the beginning, see My Fault.
