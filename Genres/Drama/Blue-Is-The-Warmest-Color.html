Blue Is the Warmest Color (2013)

About the Film

A Palme d’Or Winner About First Love

Blue Is the Warmest Color (French title: La Vie d’Adèle, Chapters 1 & 2) is a 2013 French romantic drama directed by Abdellatif Kechiche, who co-wrote the screenplay with Ghalia Lacroix. It is adapted from Julie Maroh’s graphic novel. The story follows Adèle, who meets Emma, an older student with blue-streaked hair, and falls in love over several years. The film premiered at the 2013 Cannes Film Festival, where it won the Palme d’Or, awarded to Kechiche and to actresses Adèle Exarchopoulos and Léa Seydoux, a first for the festival. Salim Kechiouche and Aurélien Recoing also star. At 179 minutes, it is rated NC-17 in the United States and contains explicit sexual content, so it is intended for adult viewers only. For another LGBTQ+ drama, try Hot Milk or The Adored.
