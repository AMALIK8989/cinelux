Our Fault (2025)

About the Film

The Final Chapter of the Culpables Trilogy

Our Fault, known in Spanish as Culpa nuestra, is a 2025 Spanish romantic drama directed by Domingo González. It is the third and final film in the Culpables trilogy, following My Fault (Culpa mía) and Your Fault (Culpa tuya). Its screenplay is credited to Mercedes Ron, Domingo González and Sofía Cuenca, and the story is based on Ron’s novel Culpa nuestra. Nicole Wallace plays Noah and Gabriel Guevara plays Nick. The synopsis describes Jenna and Lion’s wedding as the setting for the long-awaited reunion of Noah and Nick, some time after their breakup. The film was produced by Pokeepsie Films, shot alongside Your Fault, and released on Prime Video on October 16, 2025. Reviews on Rotten Tomatoes have been mixed, with 34 percent from nine critics. An English-language adaptation, Our Fault: London, has been announced, so check the title when you search for it.
