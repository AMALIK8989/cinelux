About the Film

Dance, Desire & a Second Awakening

Jeanne is a successful osteopath who has been married to David for eight years and has a son, Louis. When she meets Marie, a female dance instructor, the two quickly become friends and bond over their shared love of dance, something Jeanne had given up ten years earlier. Marie invites her to her classes, and as their friendship deepens, it develops into an affair.

Part of the Combats de femme series, the film was written and directed by Sylvie Verheyde. The story closes with Jeanne and Marie walking together along the beach.
