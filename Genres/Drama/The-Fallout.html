The Fallout (2021)

About the Film

A Teen Drama About Grief, Trauma and New Friendship

The Fallout is a 2021 American drama written and directed by Megan Park in her feature film directorial debut. It premiered at the South by Southwest festival and won the Narrative Feature Competition Audience Award there. It was released in the United States on March 17, 2021. Jenna Ortega plays Vada Cavell, a high school student dealing with the trauma of a school shooting. Her friendship with Mia Reed, played by Maddie Ziegler, helps her reinvent herself and re-evaluate her relationships with her family and friends. Niles Fitch, Will Ropp, John Ortiz, Julie Bowen, Shailene Woodley and Lumi Pollack also star, and Finneas O’Connell composed the score. The film runs 96 minutes. Critics praised Ortega’s performance, and Allmovie classifies it as an LGBT-related film. It is rated R for teen drug and alcohol use and language, and it deals with grief and trauma, so it is not recommended for younger viewers. For another LGBTQ+ film, try Hot Milk or The Adored.
