Ammonite (2020)

About the Film

A Period Romance Inspired by Mary Anning

Ammonite is a 2020 British romantic drama written and directed by Francis Lee. It is loosely inspired by the life of palaeontologist Mary Anning, played by Kate Winslet, and centers on a speculative romance between Anning and Charlotte Murchison, played by Saoirse Ronan. Anning works alone on the rugged southern coastline, and when a wealthy visitor entrusts her with the care of his wife, the two women’s bond begins to deepen. The film premiered at the Toronto International Film Festival on September 11, 2020, opened in US cinemas on November 13, 2020, and runs about two hours. Fiona Shaw, James McArdle, Gemma Jones and Alec Secăreanu also star. It is rated R for sexual content and nudity, so it is intended for adult viewers only. For another LGBTQ+ period film, try The Adored or Blue Is the Warmest Color.
