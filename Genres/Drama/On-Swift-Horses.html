On Swift Horses (2025)

About the Film

A 1950s Romantic Drama Adapted from Shannon Pufahl’s Novel

On Swift Horses is a 2024 American romantic drama directed by Daniel Minahan, with a screenplay by Bryce Kass adapted from Shannon Pufahl’s 2019 novel. It premiered at the 2024 Toronto International Film Festival, and Sony Pictures Classics released it in US theaters on April 25, 2025. Set in the 1950s, the film follows Muriel, played by Daisy Edgar-Jones, and her husband Lee, played by Will Poulter, who begin a new life in California after the Korean War. Lee’s brother Julius, played by Jacob Elordi, returns home from the war, and the brothers’ contrasting paths and Julius’s bond with Muriel drive the story. Diego Calva plays Henry and Sasha Calle also stars. The film runs 117 minutes and is rated R for sexual content, nudity and some language, so it is intended for adult viewers only. Critics praised the period look, while some found the romantic tension unconvincing. For another period romance, see Ammonite.
