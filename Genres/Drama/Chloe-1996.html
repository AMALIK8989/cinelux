Chloé (1996)

About the Film

A Serious Drama About Survival and Exploitation

Chloé is a 1996 Franco-Belgian drama directed by Dennis Berry, who co-wrote the screenplay with Christine Miller. It was made as a television film for Le Bureau and France 2, was released in France on June 11, 1996, and runs 105 minutes. Marion Cotillard plays the title role in her first leading role. Chloé, a 16-year-old, ends up on the streets after a violent dispute with her mother, and she meets Jean-Michel, played by Jean-Claude Adelin, who exploits her and draws her into prostitution. Anna Karina plays Katia, Nozha Khouadra plays Elsa, and Arache Mansour also appears. The film deals with violence and the sexual exploitation of a minor, so it is intended for adult viewers only and is best approached with care. If you are looking for another serious drama, see Hot Milk or Cocaine Godmother.
