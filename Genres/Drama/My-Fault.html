My Fault (2023)

About the Film

A Spanish Romance Adapted from a Wattpad Bestseller

My Fault (Spanish title: Culpa mía) is a 2023 Spanish romantic drama directed by Domingo González in his feature debut. He also wrote the screenplay, adapted from Mercedes Ron’s Wattpad story, the first book in her Culpables trilogy. Noah, played by Nicole Wallace, moves into the mansion of her mother’s new husband, William, and falls for Nick, her stepbrother, played by Gabriel Guevara. Marta Hazas plays Rafaella, Iván Sánchez plays William, and Iván Massagué plays Jonás. The film premiered on Amazon Prime Video on June 8, 2023, and was Prime Video’s most popular film worldwide that year. It runs 117 minutes. It is not rated and contains romantic and sexual themes along with a stepsibling storyline, so viewers should check content guidance before watching. For a similar romance, see Fifty Shades of Grey or Ammonite.
