Disobedience (2017)

About the Film

A Love Story That Tests Faith and Community

Disobedience is a 2017 romantic drama directed by Sebastián Lelio, who co-wrote the screenplay with Rebecca Lenkiewicz. It is based on Naomi Alderman’s 2006 novel and is Lelio’s English-language follow-up to his Oscar-winning film A Fantastic Woman. Rachel Weisz plays Ronit Krushka, who returns to her Orthodox Jewish community after her rabbi father’s death. There she reconnects with her childhood friend Esti Kuperman, played by Rachel McAdams, who is married to Dovid Kuperman, played by Alessandro Nivola, the rabbi’s protégé. Their rekindled relationship tests faith, community and desire. The film was produced by Weisz, Ed Guiney and Frida Torresblanco, and Bleecker Street released it in the United States in April 2018. It runs 114 minutes and is rated R for strong sexuality, so it is intended for adult viewers. Anton Lesser also appears. For another LGBTQ+ drama, see Blue Is the Warmest Color or Ammonite.
