Cocaine Godmother (2018)

About the Film

A Biopic of Miami’s Notorious Cocaine Pioneer

Cocaine Godmother is an American biographical crime drama directed by Guillermo Navarro, with a screenplay by David McKenna and Molly McAlpine. It premiered at the 2017 Camerimage festival in Poland and aired on Lifetime on January 20, 2018. The film dramatizes the life of Griselda Blanco, who entered the United States at 17 on a fake passport with her first husband Carlos. She became a pioneer of the Miami-based cocaine trade, and her willingness to use violence against rivals led to numerous assassination attempts that forced her to move her business to California. Catherine Zeta-Jones stars as Blanco, supported by Carlos Albert, Matteo Stefan, Jenny Pellicer, Spencer Borgeson and Raúl Méndez. The film runs about 100 minutes and is rated TV-14. It depicts violence and drug trafficking, so parental guidance is advised for viewers under 14. If you enjoy dramas about real people, try Blue Is the Warmest Color or Hot Milk.
