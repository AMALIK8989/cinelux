Hot Milk (2025)

About the Film

A Summer of Secrets on the Spanish Coast

Hot Milk is a 2025 British drama directed by Rebecca Lenkiewicz in her feature debut. She also wrote the screenplay, adapted from Deborah Levy’s 2016 novel of the same name, which was shortlisted for the Man Booker Prize. The film had its world premiere at the 2025 Berlin International Film Festival, where it competed for the Golden Bear, and opened in UK cinemas on July 4, 2025. Sofia, played by Emma Mackey, travels with her domineering mother Rose, played by Fiona Shaw, who is looking for a cure for a strange illness. Vicky Krieps plays Ingrid, Vincent Perez plays Gomez, and Patsy Ferran and Yann Gaël also appear. Critics praised the performances and atmosphere, though some found the pacing slow. The film is rated 15A in the UK. For another LGBTQ+ drama, try The Adored or Stuff.
