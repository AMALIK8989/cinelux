Stardust (2007)

About the Film

A Fairy-Tale Quest Based on Neil Gaiman’s Novel

Stardust is a 2007 fantasy adventure directed by Matthew Vaughn and co-written with Jane Goldman, based on Neil Gaiman’s 1999 novel. Charlie Cox plays Tristan Thorn, a young man from the village of Wall who promises his beloved a fallen star as proof of his love, and crosses into the magical kingdom of Stormhold to find one. The star turns out to be Yvaine, played by Claire Danes, and the two must evade the witch Lamia, played by Michelle Pfeiffer, and the sons of the dying King of Stormhold. Robert De Niro plays Captain Shakespeare, a pirate, and Ricky Gervais plays Ferdy the Fence. Ian McKellen narrates the film. It runs about 127 minutes, and the British Board of Film Classification says it contains moderate fantasy violence and scary moments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Young Tristan Thorn promises his beloved a fallen star as proof of his love, and crosses into a magical realm to bring one back. The star, Yvaine, turns out to be a woman, and rival forces pursue them both.

Stardust was directed by Matthew Vaughn. He co-wrote the screenplay with Jane Goldman, based on Neil Gaiman’s 1999 novel.

Charlie Cox plays Tristan Thorn and Claire Danes plays Yvaine. Michelle Pfeiffer plays Lamia, Robert De Niro plays Captain Shakespeare, and Sienna Miller plays Victoria Forester. Ricky Gervais plays Ferdy the Fence, Mark Strong plays Septimus, Jason Flemyng plays Primus, and Rupert Everett plays Secundus. Peter O'Toole plays the King of Stormhold, Ian McKellen narrates, and Henry Cavill plays Humphrey. Ben Barnes plays Young Dunstan Thorn. The rest of the cast with confirmed roles is listed on this page.

Stardust was released in 2007. Listings give a United States release date of August 10, 2007, and the BBFC classified the film on July 25, 2007.

Stardust runs about 127 minutes (2 hours 7 minutes).
