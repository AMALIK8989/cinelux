Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Man’s Chest (2006)

About the Film

The Second Film in the Pirates of the Caribbean Franchise

Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Man’s Chest is a 2006 American fantasy action-adventure film directed by Gore Verbinski, with a screenplay by Ted Elliott and Terry Rossio. It is the sequel to Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl. When the ghostly Davy Jones, played by Bill Nighy, comes to collect a blood debt, Captain Jack Sparrow, played by Johnny Depp, is pulled into a race for the Dead Man’s Chest. Will Turner and Elizabeth Swann, played by Orlando Bloom and Keira Knightley, face interference from Lord Cutler Beckett, played by Tom Hollander, and Will learns of his long-lost father, Bootstrap Bill Turner, played by Stellan Skarsgård. Jack Davenport, Jonathan Pryce, Kevin R. McNally, Naomie Harris and Geoffrey Rush also star. The film was released in US theaters on July 7, 2006, by Buena Vista Pictures, runs 151 minutes and is rated PG-13. It was followed by At World’s End (2007). For the first film, see Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl.

When the ghostly Davy Jones comes to collect a blood debt, Captain Jack Sparrow is pulled into a race for the Dead Man’s Chest. Meanwhile, Will Turner and Elizabeth Swann face interference from Lord Cutler Beckett, who wants Will to acquire Jack’s compass.

Gore Verbinski directed the film. Ted Elliott and Terry Rossio wrote the screenplay, and the characters were created by Stuart Beattie and Jay Wolpert.

Johnny Depp plays Captain Jack Sparrow and Orlando Bloom plays Will Turner. Keira Knightley plays Elizabeth Swann, Bill Nighy plays Davy Jones and Jack Davenport plays Commodore Norrington. Jonathan Pryce plays Governor Weatherby Swann, Kevin R. McNally plays Gibbs and Naomie Harris plays Tia Dalma. Tom Hollander plays Cutler Beckett, Stellan Skarsgård plays Bootstrap Bill Turner, David Bailie plays Cotton and Geoffrey Rush plays Barbossa. Martin Klebba plays Marty, Buddy Dolan plays Henge, Jonathan Linsley plays Ogilvey and Andy Beckwith plays Clacker.
