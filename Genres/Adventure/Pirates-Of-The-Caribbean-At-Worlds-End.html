Pirates of the Caribbean: At World’s End (2007)

About the Film

The Final Chapter of the Original Pirates Trilogy

Pirates of the Caribbean: At World’s End is a 2007 American fantasy action-adventure film directed by Gore Verbinski, with a screenplay by Ted Elliott and Terry Rossio. It is the third film in the franchise and completes the original trilogy after Dead Man’s Chest (2006). Captain Jack Sparrow, played by Johnny Depp, is trapped in Davy Jones’ Locker. Will Turner and Elizabeth Swann, played by Orlando Bloom and Keira Knightley, join forces with Captain Hector Barbossa, played by Geoffrey Rush, to rescue him. Their uneasy alliance faces Lord Cutler Beckett, played by Tom Hollander, who controls Davy Jones and the Flying Dutchman. Chow Yun-Fat plays Captain Sao Feng, and Bill Nighy plays Davy Jones. Jack Davenport, Jonathan Pryce, Stellan Skarsgård, Kevin R. McNally and Mackenzie Crook also star. The film was released in US theaters on May 25, 2007, by Buena Vista Pictures, runs 169 minutes and is rated PG-13. Critics praised the performances, score and visual effects, but criticized the plot, pacing and length. For the previous film, see Pirates of the Caribbean: Dead Man’s Chest.
