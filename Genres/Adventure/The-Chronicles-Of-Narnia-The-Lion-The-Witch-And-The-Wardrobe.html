About the Film

The First Narnia Adventure on Screen

The Chronicles of Narnia: The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe is a 2005 fantasy adventure directed by Andrew Adamson and based on C. S. Lewis’s 1950 children’s novel. Georgie Henley plays Lucy Pevensie, the youngest of four siblings who discover Narnia through a wardrobe. Skandar Keynes plays Edmund, William Moseley plays Peter, and Anna Popplewell plays Susan. In Narnia, Tilda Swinton plays Jadis, the White Witch, who has kept the land in eternal winter, and James McAvoy plays Mr. Tumnus, the faun who first befriends Lucy. Liam Neeson voices Aslan, the lion who leads the fight against the Witch. The screenplay is credited to Ann Peacock, Andrew Adamson, Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely. Walt Disney Pictures released the film on December 9, 2005. It runs about 143 minutes and is rated PG.

Frequently Asked Questions

Four siblings, Lucy, Edmund, Susan and Peter Pevensie, step through a wardrobe into Narnia, a land where the White Witch, Jadis, has made it always winter. They join the lion Aslan in the fight against her.

The Chronicles of Narnia: The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe was directed by Andrew Adamson. The screenplay is credited to Ann Peacock, Andrew Adamson, Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely.

Georgie Henley plays Lucy Pevensie, Skandar Keynes plays Edmund Pevensie, William Moseley plays Peter Pevensie, and Anna Popplewell plays Susan Pevensie. Tilda Swinton plays Jadis, The White Witch, and James McAvoy plays Mr. Tumnus. Liam Neeson voices Aslan, Ray Winstone and Dawn French voice Mr. and Mrs. Beaver, and Rupert Everett voices Mr. Fox. Sophie Winkleman plays Older Susan, and the full list of confirmed roles appears on this page.

The Chronicles of Narnia: The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe was released on December 9, 2005, by Walt Disney Pictures.

The film runs about 143 minutes (2 hours 23 minutes), according to the IMDb listing.

The Chronicles of Narnia is rated PG by the MPAA.
