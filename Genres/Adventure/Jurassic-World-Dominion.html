Jurassic World: Dominion (2022)

About the Film

The Final Chapter of the Jurassic World Trilogy

Jurassic World: Dominion is a 2022 American action-adventure science fiction film directed by Colin Trevorrow, who co-wrote the screenplay with Emily Carmichael. The story is by Trevorrow and Derek Connolly, and the characters are based on Michael Crichton’s creations. It is the sixth film in the Jurassic franchise and the final chapter of the Jurassic World trilogy. Set four years after Isla Nublar was destroyed, the film follows dinosaurs living alongside humans around the world. Chris Pratt and Bryce Dallas Howard return as Owen Grady and Claire Dearing, joined by the original Jurassic Park stars Laura Dern, Sam Neill and Jeff Goldblum. DeWanda Wise, Mamoudou Athie, Isabella Sermon, Campbell Scott, BD Wong and Omar Sy also star. The film was released in US theaters on June 10, 2022, by Universal Pictures, runs 147 minutes and is rated PG-13. For the previous film, see Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom.
