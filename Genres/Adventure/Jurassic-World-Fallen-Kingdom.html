Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom (2018)

About the Film

The Fifth Film in the Jurassic Park Franchise

Jurassic World: Fallen Kingdom is a 2018 American-Spanish science fiction adventure film directed by J.A. Bayona. It is the sequel to Jurassic World (2015) and the fifth film in the Jurassic Park franchise. Derek Connolly and Colin Trevorrow wrote the screenplay, based on characters created by Michael Crichton, and Michael Giacchino composed the score. When the island’s dormant volcano begins to erupt, Owen Grady and Claire Dearing, played by Chris Pratt and Bryce Dallas Howard, lead a campaign to rescue the remaining dinosaurs. Rafe Spall, Justice Smith, Daniella Pineda, James Cromwell, Toby Jones, Ted Levine, Geraldine Chaplin and Isabella Sermon also star, and BD Wong and Jeff Goldblum return from the earlier films. The film opened in US theaters on June 22, 2018, through Universal Pictures, runs 128 minutes and is rated PG-13. Critics praised the spectacle, though some found the lead characters less engaging. For the earlier film in the series, see Jurassic World.
