On the Road (2012)

About the Film

Walter Salles’ Adaptation of Jack Kerouac’s Beat Classic

On the Road is a 2012 adventure drama directed by Walter Salles. The screenplay is by José Rivera, adapted from Jack Kerouac’s 1957 novel, which draws on his travels with Neal Cassady. Sam Riley plays Sal Paradise, an aspiring writer, and Garrett Hedlund plays Dean Moriarty, a restless figure based on Cassady. Kristen Stewart plays Marylou, Amy Adams plays Jane and Tom Sturridge plays Carlo Marx. Kirsten Dunst plays Camille Moriarty and Viggo Mortensen plays Old Bull Lee. Alice Braga, Elisabeth Moss, Danny Morgan, Steve Buscemi and Terrence Howard also appear. The film was produced by Francis Ford Coppola, Nathanael Karmitz, Charles Gillibert and Rebecca Yeldham, with cinematography by Éric Gautier and a score by Gustavo Santaolalla. IFC Films distributed it in US theaters in December 2012. It runs 124 minutes and is rated R. For another road-trip story about searching for freedom, see Violet.
