Jurassic World (2015)

About the Film

The Fourth Film in the Jurassic Park Franchise

Jurassic World is a 2015 American science fiction action-adventure film directed by Colin Trevorrow, who co-wrote the screenplay with Derek Connolly. The story is by Rick Jaffa and Amanda Silver, and the characters are based on Michael Crichton’s creations. The film takes place on Isla Nublar, where the dinosaur park is now fully operational. To revive visitor interest, the park engineers a new hybrid dinosaur, the Indominus rex, which escapes containment and goes on a killing spree. Chris Pratt plays Owen Grady and Bryce Dallas Howard plays Claire Dearing, who must rescue her nephews Zach and Gray, played by Nick Robinson and Ty Simpkins. Irrfan Khan, Vincent D’Onofrio, Omar Sy and BD Wong also star. The film opened in US theaters on June 12, 2015, runs 124 minutes and is rated PG-13 for intense science fiction violence and peril. IMDb lists 15 wins and 58 nominations. For another action-driven film, see Camp X-Ray.

Frequently Asked Questions

The fully operational dinosaur park on Isla Nublar struggles to keep visitors interested, so its engineers create a genetically engineered hybrid, the Indominus rex. It escapes containment and goes on a killing spree, and Owen Grady and Claire Dearing must stop it and rescue the visitors, including Claire’s nephews.

Colin Trevorrow directed the film. He co-wrote the screenplay with Derek Connolly, and the story was by Rick Jaffa and Amanda Silver. The characters are based on Michael Crichton’s creations.

Chris Pratt plays Owen Grady and Bryce Dallas Howard plays Claire Dearing. Irrfan Khan plays Simon Masrani, Vincent D’Onofrio plays Vic Hoskins, Ty Simpkins plays Gray Mitchell, Nick Robinson plays Zach Mitchell, Jake Johnson plays Lowery Cruz, Omar Sy plays Barry, BD Wong plays Dr. Henry Wu and Judy Greer plays Karen Mitchell. The full cast list on this page includes Lauren Lapkus, Brian Tee, Katie McGrath, Andy Buckley, Martin Klebba, Jimmy Fallon, Colin Trevorrow, Brad Bird and others.

Jurassic World opened in US theaters on June 12, 2015, and was released for home viewing on October 20, 2015.

The film runs 124 minutes, or 2 hours and 4 minutes.

Yes. It is the fourth film in the Jurassic Park franchise, following the original Jurassic Park (1993).

The film is rated PG-13 in the United States for intense science fiction violence and peril. Parents are strongly cautioned, and younger children may find it frightening.
