Pirates of the Caribbean: On Stranger Tides (2011)

About the Film

The Fourth Voyage Into the Pirate Series

Pirates of the Caribbean: On Stranger Tides is a 2011 American fantasy swashbuckler directed by Rob Marshall. It is the fourth film in the Pirates of the Caribbean series, following At World’s End (2007), and it was written by Ted Elliott and Terry Rossio. The story is loosely based on Tim Powers’s 1987 novel On Stranger Tides. Johnny Depp returns as Captain Jack Sparrow, joined by Geoffrey Rush as Hector Barbossa. Penélope Cruz plays Angelica, Ian McShane plays Blackbeard, and Sam Claflin plays Philip Swift. Kevin McNally reprises Joshamee Gibbs, and Astrid Bergès-Frisbey plays the mermaid Syrena. The film premiered at Disneyland Resort on May 7, 2011, and was released in the United States on May 20, 2011, by Walt Disney Studios Motion Pictures. It runs 136 minutes and is rated PG-13. Hans Zimmer composed the score, and Jerry Bruckheimer produced. It became the second film in the franchise to gross more than $1 billion worldwide. For another swashbuckling adventure with a fantastical twist, try Camp X-Ray or Fifty Shades Darker.
