Uncharted (2022)

About the Film

A Treasure Hunt Adapted From the PlayStation Game

Uncharted is a 2022 action-adventure film directed by Ruben Fleischer and based on Naughty Dog’s PlayStation video game series. The screenplay is credited to Rafe Judkins, Art Marcum and Matt Holloway. Tom Holland plays Nathan Drake, a street-smart thief who is recruited by Victor “Sully” Sullivan, played by Mark Wahlberg. Together they race to recover a fortune lost by Ferdinand Magellan 500 years ago, a race that puts them against the corrupt billionaire Santiago Moncada, played by Antonio Banderas, and the mercenary Jo Braddock, played by Tati Gabrielle. Sophia Ali plays Chloe Frazer. The film was released by Columbia Pictures and PlayStation Productions on February 18, 2022, and runs about 116 minutes. It is rated PG-13.

Frequently Asked Questions

Street-smart thief Nathan Drake is recruited by treasure hunter Victor “Sully” Sullivan to recover a fortune lost by Ferdinand Magellan 500 years ago. They race against the ruthless Santiago Moncada, who believes he and his family are the rightful heirs.

Uncharted was directed by Ruben Fleischer. The screenplay is credited to Rafe Judkins, Art Marcum and Matt Holloway.

The film stars Tom Holland as Nathan Drake and Mark Wahlberg as Victor Sullivan. Antonio Banderas plays Santiago Moncada, Sophia Ali plays Chloe Frazer, and Tati Gabrielle plays Jo Braddock. Steven Waddington plays The Scotsman, Rudy Pankow plays Young Sam, Tiernan Jones plays Young Nate, Pingi Moli plays Hugo, Patricia Meeden plays a Spanish woman in a club, and Peter Seaton-Clark plays the auctioneer.

Uncharted was released in the United States on February 18, 2022, by Columbia Pictures and PlayStation Productions.

Uncharted runs about 116 minutes (1 hour 56 minutes).

Uncharted is rated PG-13 by the MPAA.
