Jurassic Park (1993)

About the Film

Where Science and Spectacle Meet on Isla Nublar

Jurassic Park is a 1993 science fiction adventure directed by Steven Spielberg. It is based on Michael Crichton’s 1990 novel, and Crichton and David Koepp wrote the screenplay. John Hammond, the billionaire behind InGen, invites paleontologists Alan Grant and Ellie Sattler, along with mathematician Ian Malcolm, to tour his island park of cloned dinosaurs before it opens. Sam Neill plays Grant, Laura Dern plays Sattler, and Jeff Goldblum plays Malcolm, while Richard Attenborough plays Hammond. When a disgruntled programmer sabotages the park’s systems, the visitors must survive the dinosaurs that break free. The film premiered in Washington, D.C., on June 9, 1993, and was released in the United States on June 11, 1993, by Universal Pictures and Amblin Entertainment. It runs 127 minutes and is rated PG-13. John Williams composed the score, and the film is widely credited with advancing computer-generated visual effects. It grossed more than $1 billion worldwide and was selected for the National Film Registry in 2018. For another adventure with a fantastical twist, try Pirates of the Caribbean: On Stranger Tides or Camp X-Ray.
