About the Film

A New Breed of Vampire Emerges

In this sequel, Blade continues his war against the undead until a crisis forces him into an uneasy partnership with the Bloodpack, a squad of vampire warriors once trained to kill him. Together they track the Reapers, a mutated breed of super-vampires whose hunger threatens humans and vampires alike.
