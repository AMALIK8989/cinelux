About the Film

A Cyber-Caper Between Shanghai and Kuala Lumpur

Reborn is a 2018 Chinese action thriller directed by Li Hailong. Han Geng plays Li Haoming, a hacker who now lives mostly inside virtual games. Rhydian Vaughan plays Qiao Fei, a mercenary hacker, and Li Yuan plays Su Yi, his partner. Tomohisa Yamashita plays Mori Takeshi, the businessman who hires them, and Liu Kai-Chi plays Detective Chow, a Hong Kong officer trying to arrest Qiao Fei. The story moves between Shanghai and Kuala Lumpur as the crew sets out to hack a security system. The screenplay is credited to Erica Li, Chi Liu, Liang Su and Qiao Yu. The film was released in China on August 3, 2018, and runs about 99 minutes. Reviews were mixed, with critics pointing to its heavy use of technology and its fight scenes.
