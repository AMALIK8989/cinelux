Argylle (2024)

About the Film

An Action-Comedy Spy Thriller from the Director of Kingsman

Argylle is a 2024 action-comedy directed and produced by Matthew Vaughn, from a screenplay by Jason Fuchs. Elly Conway, played by Bryce Dallas Howard, is a reclusive author of espionage novels about a secret agent named Argylle, played by Henry Cavill. When the plots of her books begin to mirror the covert actions of a real spy organization, she teams up with Aidan Wilde, a cat-allergic spy played by Sam Rockwell, and races across the world with her cat Alfie. The ensemble includes Bryan Cranston as Director Ritter, Catherine O’Hara as Ruth, Sofia Boutella as Saba Al-Badr, Dua Lipa as LaGrange, Ariana DeBose as Keira, and John Cena as Wyatt. The film premiered in London on January 24, 2024, and was released in the United States on February 2, 2024. It runs 139 minutes and is rated PG-13 for strong violence and action and some strong language. It grossed about $96.2 million and received mostly negative reviews, although its plot twists and cast drew interest. Vaughn has said he plans a prequel and a shared-universe crossover with Kingsman. For a similar film, see Kingsman: The Secret Service or Red Notice.
