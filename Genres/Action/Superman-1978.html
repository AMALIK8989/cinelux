Superman (1978)

About the Film

The Film That Launched the Modern Superhero Blockbuster

Superman is a 1978 superhero film directed by Richard Donner and based on the DC Comics character created by Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster. Christopher Reeve plays Superman and his alter ego, Clark Kent, and Margot Kidder plays Lois Lane, the reporter who becomes his love interest. Gene Hackman plays the villain Lex Luthor, and Marlon Brando plays Jor-El, Superman’s father on Krypton. The story covers Clark’s upbringing in Smallville with Glenn Ford as Pa Kent and Phyllis Thaxter as Ma Kent, his move to Metropolis, and his battle with Luthor. Terence Stamp plays General Zod, whose attack from the Phantom Zone is part of the film’s finale. The screenplay credits include Mario Puzo, David Newman, Leslie Newman, Robert Benton and Tom Mankiewicz. The film was released in the United States on December 15, 1978, runs about 143 minutes, and is rated PG for peril, some mild sensuality and language.

Frequently Asked Questions

Raised by farmers Jonathan and Martha Kent, young Clark Kent discovers his superhuman powers and moves to Metropolis to fight evil as Superman. He battles the villain Lex Luthor, while as a reporter he tries to win over his colleague Lois Lane.

Superman was directed by Richard Donner. The screenplay credits include Mario Puzo, David Newman, Leslie Newman, Robert Benton and Tom Mankiewicz, with the characters created by Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster.

Christopher Reeve plays Superman and Clark Kent, and Margot Kidder plays Lois Lane. Gene Hackman plays Lex Luthor, and Marlon Brando plays Jor-El. Ned Beatty plays Otis, Glenn Ford plays Pa Kent, and Terence Stamp plays General Zod. Jackie Cooper plays Perry White, Valerie Perrine plays Eve Teschmacher, and Jeff East plays Young Clark Kent. The full list of confirmed roles appears on this page.

Superman was released in the United States on December 15, 1978.

Superman runs about 143 minutes (2 hours 23 minutes), according to the Metacritic and Laemmle listings.

Superman is rated PG by the MPAA for peril, some mild sensuality and language.
