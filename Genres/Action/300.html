Yes, loosely. The film dramatizes the Battle of Thermopylae in 480 BC, where a small Greek force led by King Leonidas held off the much larger Persian army. The story is heavily stylized for effect and adapted from Frank Miller's graphic novel rather than presented as a strict historical record.

300 carries an R rating for graphic war violence and some sexual content. It's intended for mature audiences and isn't recommended for younger viewers.

The film was directed by Zack Snyder, known for his distinctive slow-motion, high-contrast visual style. It stars Gerard Butler as King Leonidas, alongside Lena Headey, David Wenham, and Rodrigo Santoro.

The film runs for approximately 1 hour and 57 minutes. A follow-up, 300: Rise of an Empire, was released in 2014, expanding the story with a parallel naval battle set around the same conflict.
