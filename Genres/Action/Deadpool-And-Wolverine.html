Deadpool & Wolverine (2024)

About the Film

A Marvel Multiverse Action-Comedy and the First R-Rated MCU Film

Deadpool & Wolverine is a 2024 Marvel superhero action-comedy directed by Shawn Levy. The screenplay was written by Ryan Reynolds, Rhett Reese, Paul Wernick, Zeb Wells and Shawn Levy. Ryan Reynolds plays Wade Wilson, the mercenary known as Deadpool, and Hugh Jackman plays Logan, the Wolverine. When Wade’s homeworld faces an existential threat from the Time Variance Authority, he must reluctantly team up with a Wolverine from another universe. The cast also includes Emma Corrin as Cassandra Nova, Matthew Macfadyen as Mr. Paradox, Dafne Keen as Laura, Jon Favreau as Happy Hogan, and returning characters such as Jennifer Garner’s Elektra, Wesley Snipes’s Blade, Channing Tatum’s Gambit and Chris Evans’s Johnny Storm. The film premiered on July 22, 2024, and was released on July 26, 2024. It runs 128 minutes and is rated R, making it the first R-rated Marvel Cinematic Universe film. It grossed about $1.338 billion worldwide. For a similar superhero film, see Deadpool or Logan.
