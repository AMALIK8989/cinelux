Blade II (2002)

About the Film

Blade and the Bloodpack Face a Deadlier Enemy

Directed by Guillermo del Toro and written by David S. Goyer, Blade II expands the vampire-hunter’s world with a darker biological threat. The Reapers are a mutated strain of vampires whose distinctive feeding method and rapid spread place the entire vampire population at risk. To find their source, Blade must work with the Bloodpack, a specialist unit led by Nyssa and assembled by the vampire council. The alliance is unstable: the Bloodpack was created to eliminate Blade, while Blade has no reason to trust the vampires now fighting beside him. Their hunt leads to Nomak, the first and most dangerous Reaper, and a conspiracy that reaches into the highest levels of vampire society.
