About the Film

The Ancient Secret Behind the Transformers

Transformers: Revenge of the Fallen (2009) is the second live-action film in the Transformers series, directed by Michael Bay. Two years after the Battle of Mission City, Sam Witwicky is preparing for college when a shard of the AllSpark begins revealing mysterious Cybertronian symbols. The Decepticons, led by a revived Megatron and the ancient Fallen, pursue the knowledge hidden in Sam’s mind. With Optimus Prime, Bumblebee and the Autobots facing a threat tied to the earliest history of their species, Sam and his allies must uncover the secret before the Fallen can use an ancient machine to devastate Earth.
