About the Film

A War Fought on the Water

Based on Frank Miller's graphic novel Xerxes, this companion to 300 shifts the battlefield to the sea. As Xerxes' forces bear down on Greece, Themistokles attempts to rally the divided Greek city-states while facing the Persian navy's most formidable commander — Artemisia, a woman driven by vengeance and unmatched tactical brilliance.
