Transformers (2007)

About the Film

The Live-Action Start of the Transformers Franchise

Transformers is a 2007 science fiction action film directed by Michael Bay, based on the Hasbro toy line. The screenplay is credited to Roberto Orci, Alex Kurtzman and John Rogers. Shia LaBeouf plays Sam Witwicky, a high school student whose new pair of glasses holds the key to a war between the Autobots, led by Optimus Prime, and the Decepticons, led by Megatron. Megan Fox plays Mikaela Banes, Josh Duhamel plays Captain William Lennox, and Tyrese Gibson plays Tech Sergeant Epps. Peter Cullen voices Optimus Prime, and Hugo Weaving voices Megatron. The film was released in the United States on June 27, 2007, and runs about 144 minutes. It is rated PG-13 by the MPAA and 12 by the British Board of Film Classification for moderate action violence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sam Witwicky, a teenager who has inherited a pair of glasses that hold the key to a war between two alien robot factions, learns that the Autobots, led by Optimus Prime, and the Decepticons, led by Megatron, are fighting for the Allspark on Earth. Only Sam can help the Autobots win.

Transformers was directed by Michael Bay. The screenplay is credited to Roberto Orci, Alex Kurtzman and John Rogers.

Shia LaBeouf plays Sam Witwicky, and Megan Fox plays Mikaela Banes. Josh Duhamel plays Captain William Lennox, and Tyrese Gibson plays Tech Sergeant Epps. Rachael Taylor plays Maggie Madsen, Anthony Anderson plays Glen Whitmann, and John Turturro plays Agent Simmons. Jon Voight plays Defense Secretary John Keller. Peter Cullen voices Optimus Prime, and Hugo Weaving voices Megatron. The full list of confirmed roles appears on this page.

Transformers was released in the United States on June 27, 2007.
