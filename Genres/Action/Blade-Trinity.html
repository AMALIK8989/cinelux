Blade: Trinity (2004)

About the Film

The Daywalker Faces the Original Vampire

Blade: Trinity brings the Daywalker into a deadly confrontation that threatens to expose the hidden vampire world. Framed for a string of murders, Blade is captured by the FBI and pushed into an uneasy alliance with the Nightstalkers, a secret band of human vampire hunters. Alongside Abigail Whistler and Hannibal King, he takes the fight to Danica Talos and Drake, the ancient vampire known as Dracula. The film closes the original Blade trilogy with a mix of martial-arts combat, supernatural horror, science-fiction action and the franchise’s signature vampire mythology.
