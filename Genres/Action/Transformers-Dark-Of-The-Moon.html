Transformers: Dark of the Moon (2011)

About the Film

The Moon Mission That Brought the Saga to Earth’s Orbit

Transformers: Dark of the Moon is a 2011 science fiction action film directed by Michael Bay and written by Ehren Kruger. It is the third live-action film in the Transformers franchise. Shia LaBeouf returns as Sam Witwicky, who is now struggling to find a job after college, while Rosie Huntington-Whiteley plays Carly Spencer, his new girlfriend. Josh Duhamel plays William Lennox, and Tyrese Gibson plays Robert Epps. Frances McDormand plays Charlotte Mearing, a government official, and John Malkovich plays Bruce Brazos, Sam’s boss. Peter Cullen voices Optimus Prime, Hugo Weaving voices Megatron, and Leonard Nimoy voices Sentinel Prime. The film was released in the United States on June 29, 2011, runs about 154 minutes, and is rated PG-13 by the MPAA. It was shot in 3D and became one of the highest-grossing films of 2011.

After mysterious Autobot technology is found in Chernobyl, Optimus Prime leads a mission to the dark side of the Moon, where he discovers his predecessor, Sentinel Prime. Sam Witwicky must help the Autobots stop a Decepticon plot to destroy Earth.

Transformers: Dark of the Moon was directed by Michael Bay. Ehren Kruger wrote the screenplay.

Shia LaBeouf plays Sam Witwicky, and Rosie Huntington-Whiteley plays Carly Spencer. Tyrese Gibson plays Robert Epps, and Josh Duhamel plays William Lennox. John Turturro plays Seymour Simmons, Patrick Dempsey plays Dylan Gould, and Frances McDormand plays Charlotte Mearing. John Malkovich plays Bruce Brazos, and Ken Jeong plays Jerry Wang. Peter Cullen voices Optimus Prime, Hugo Weaving voices Megatron, and Leonard Nimoy voices Sentinel Prime. The full list of confirmed roles appears on this page.

Transformers: Dark of the Moon was released in the United States on June 29, 2011.

Transformers: Dark of the Moon runs about 154 minutes (2 hours 34 minutes), according to the IMDb and Common Sense Media listings. Some listings show 157 minutes.

Transformers: Dark of the Moon is rated PG-13 by the MPAA.
