The New Mutants (2020)

About the Film

The X-Men Spin-Off That Went Straight to Horror

The New Mutants is a 2020 American superhero horror film based on the Marvel Comics team of the same name. It was directed by Josh Boone, who wrote the screenplay with Knate Lee, and it was released in the United States on August 28, 2020. The film follows five young mutants held at a secret institution run by Dr. Cecilia Reyes, played by Alice Braga. Maisie Williams plays Rahne Sinclair, the werewolf-like Wolfsbane, and Anya Taylor-Joy plays Illyana Rasputin, the sorcerer Magik. Charlie Heaton plays Sam Guthrie, who becomes Cannonball, and Blu Hunt plays Dani Moonstar, who becomes Mirage. Henry Zaga plays Roberto da Costa, the solar-powered Sunspot. The film runs about 94 minutes and is rated PG-13 for violent content, some disturbing images, strong language and suggestive material.

Frequently Asked Questions

Five teenage mutants, Dani Moonstar, Rahne Sinclair, Sam Guthrie, Roberto da Costa and Illyana Rasputin, are held at a secret institution run by Dr. Cecilia Reyes. Told that treatment will cure their powers, they begin to question why they are being held and who is trying to destroy them.

The New Mutants was directed by Josh Boone, who wrote the screenplay with Knate Lee. The characters were created by Chris Claremont and Bob McLeod.

Maisie Williams plays Rahne Sinclair / Wolfsbane, and Anya Taylor-Joy plays Illyana Rasputin / Magik. Charlie Heaton plays Sam Guthrie / Cannonball, and Blu Hunt plays Danielle Moonstar / Mirage. Henry Zaga plays Roberto da Costa / Sunspot, and Alice Braga plays Dr. Cecilia Reyes. Adam Beach plays Dani's father, and Thomas Kee plays Thomas Guthrie. The full list of confirmed roles appears on this page.

The New Mutants was released in the United States on August 28, 2020. It reached home release on November 17, 2020.

The New Mutants runs about 94 minutes, according to the Metacritic listing. Apple TV lists 1 hour 34 minutes.

The New Mutants is rated PG-13 by the MPAA for violent content, some disturbing and bloody images, strong language, thematic elements and suggestive material.
