About the Film

A Night Ride Into the Vampire Underworld

Night Teeth follows Benny, a college student who takes a late-night chauffeur job to earn extra money. Filling in for his older brother, Jay, he agrees to drive Blaire and Zoe between exclusive Los Angeles parties. The passengers seem glamorous and mysterious, but Benny soon discovers that they are vampires — and that the city's nightlife hides a fragile peace between humans and the supernatural.

As the night unfolds, Benny becomes entangled in a struggle involving rival vampire factions and the people tasked with protecting the human world. With danger closing in and sunrise approaching, he must work out whom he can trust while trying to survive the journey.

Directed by Adam Randall and written by Brent Dillon, Night Teeth (2021) blends vampire horror, urban fantasy and thriller elements against the backdrop of Los Angeles after dark. Its story centres on a seemingly ordinary driver whose one-night job exposes him to a secret world operating just beyond the city's neon-lit streets.

What is Night Teeth about?

At its heart, Night Teeth is a supernatural thriller about a chauffeur caught between two worlds. Benny's passengers are not simply going from club to club: their journey draws him into a hidden conflict between vampires and the humans who have maintained an uneasy truce with them. The film uses the city-night setting, secret alliances and escalating danger to drive its story.
