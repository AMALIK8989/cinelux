Transformers: Age of Extinction (2014)

About the Film

A New Era of Transformers

Transformers: Age of Extinction (2014) is the fourth live-action film in the Transformers series. Set several years after the Battle of Chicago, the story follows Cade Yeager, a resourceful Texas inventor who finds the damaged Optimus Prime while salvaging an old truck. Their discovery puts Cade, his daughter Tessa and her boyfriend Shane in the sights of a covert government unit and a ruthless alien bounty hunter. Meanwhile, a powerful corporation is using captured Cybertronian technology to create a new generation of Transformers. With the Autobots hunted and Earth facing another invasion, Optimus Prime must reunite his allies and call on the legendary Dinobots. Directed by Michael Bay and written by Ehren Kruger, the film combines large-scale action, science-fiction spectacle and globe-spanning set pieces.

Frequently Asked Questions

After the Battle of Chicago, inventor Cade Yeager discovers a damaged Optimus Prime and becomes caught in a global hunt for the Autobots, while a powerful new threat emerges and the Dinobots join the fight.

The film was released in the United States on 27 June 2014. It had earlier international premieres, including Hong Kong on 19 June 2014.

Transformers: Age of Extinction was directed by Michael Bay.

The screenplay was written by Ehren Kruger, based on the Transformers characters and toy line created by Hasbro.

The theatrical runtime is approximately 165 minutes, or 2 hours and 45 minutes.

The principal cast includes Mark Wahlberg, Nicola Peltz, Jack Reynor, Stanley Tucci, Kelsey Grammer, Li Bingbing, T.J. Miller, Sophia Myles and Titus Welliver.

The Dinobots are powerful Transformers who take dinosaur-like forms. Optimus Prime seeks their help during the film’s climactic battle.

Yes. It is the fourth live-action Transformers film, following Transformers: Dark of the Moon (2011) and preceding Transformers: The Last Knight (2017).
