Lizzie (2018)

About the Film

A Dark Story Behind the Borden House

Set in Fall River, Massachusetts, in 1892, Lizzie explores the troubled world of Lizzie Borden, a woman living under the strict authority of her father, Andrew Borden. Her life changes when Bridget Sullivan, a new Irish maid, arrives at the family home. The two develop a close and increasingly intimate relationship as the atmosphere inside the household becomes more oppressive.

Directed by Craig William Macneill and written by Bryce Kass, the film revisits one of America's most notorious historical murder cases. Rather than presenting the story as a conventional crime procedural, Lizzie focuses on domestic power, isolation, repression, class, desire and the relationships that develop behind the closed doors of the Borden home.

Chloë Sevigny leads the film as Lizzie Borden, with Kristen Stewart portraying Bridget Sullivan. Their performances anchor a period thriller that builds toward the infamous 1892 murders of Andrew and Abby Borden. The film offers a dramatic interpretation of the events and the people surrounding the case, rather than a documentary account.
