We Kill Them All (2025)

About the Film

A Family Reunion Turns Into a Fight for Survival

We Kill Them All is a 2025 Canadian thriller directed by Jeremy Drummond. The story centres on a young woman who travels to a secluded resort to reconnect with her estranged father, a man whose criminal past has left him surrounded by dangerous enemies.

She does not arrive alone. Her girlfriend accompanies her, and what should have been an opportunity to repair a broken family relationship quickly becomes something far more threatening. The men connected to her father's past appear, turning the remote getaway into a place where survival becomes the only priority.

With danger closing in and few safe places to turn, the women must navigate the escalating threat and find a way to escape. The isolated setting, criminal connections and strained family relationships form the central tension of the film.

Featuring Chloe McClay, Emma Newton and Leo Fafard, We Kill Them All blends thriller and mystery elements with a survival-focused story. The film explores loyalty, family conflict, relationships and the consequences of being pulled into someone else's dangerous past.

Directed by Jeremy Drummond and written by Nicole Field, the film is a Canadian production from Trilight Entertainment. Its cast also includes Kent Allen, Thomas Perras, Michasha Armstrong, Daniel Ford Beavis, Ivy Sava and Clare Middleton.
