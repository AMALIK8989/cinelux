About the Film

A Friendship Turns Dangerous

Poison Ivy follows Sylvie Cooper, a quiet teenager who forms a close bond with Ivy, a confident and alluring new friend. Ivy grows increasingly obsessed with Sylvie’s family and begins manipulating those around her, turning an apparently exciting friendship into a psychological battle within the Cooper household.
