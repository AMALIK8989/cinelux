About the Film

A Story of Love, Isolation and Violence

Monster (2003) is a biographical crime drama written and directed by Patty Jenkins. The film follows Aileen Wuornos, portrayed by Charlize Theron, a woman struggling to find stability and acceptance while living in Florida. Her life takes a different direction after she meets Selby Wall, played by Christina Ricci, and the two begin an intense relationship.

As Aileen attempts to build a future with Selby, financial pressure, difficult circumstances and a series of violent encounters push their relationship into increasingly dangerous territory. The film explores the emotional complexity of its central characters while tracing the events that surround Wuornos' crimes.

Rather than presenting itself as a documentary, Monster is a dramatic interpretation inspired by real events. Patty Jenkins places the characters and their relationship at the center of the narrative, creating a character-driven film about isolation, desperation, attachment and the consequences of violence.

Charlize Theron's Award-Winning Performance

Charlize Theron leads the film as Aileen Wuornos, delivering a transformative performance that became one of the defining roles of her career. Christina Ricci co-stars as Selby Wall, bringing emotional depth to the complicated relationship at the center of the story.

Monster received widespread recognition, particularly for Theron's performance, and earned her the Academy Award for Best Actress. The film also marked an important feature-directing debut for Patty Jenkins.
