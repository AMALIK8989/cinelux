Depravity (2024)

About the Film

A Suspicious Neighbor. A Deadly Discovery.

Depravity (2024) is a horror thriller written and directed by Paul Tamasy. The story follows three residents who become increasingly suspicious of their reclusive neighbor, believing that he may be responsible for a series of brutal crimes. Their curiosity leads them into his apartment, where they discover an unexpected fortune in stolen artwork. What begins as an attempt to uncover the truth quickly becomes a terrifying struggle for survival.

Blending mystery, psychological tension and survival-horror elements, Depravity builds its suspense around suspicion, deception and the danger of discovering something that was never meant to be found. The film features Taylor John Smith, Victoria Justice, Devon Ross, Alex Roe, Adam Lazarre-White, Sasha Luss and Dermot Mulroney.
