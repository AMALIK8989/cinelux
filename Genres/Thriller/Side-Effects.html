Side Effects (2013)

About the Film

A Prescription for Deception

Directed by Steven Soderbergh and written by Scott Z. Burns, Side Effects is a psychological thriller that turns the world of psychiatric treatment into a tense mystery. Emily Taylor is struggling with anxiety as her husband Martin prepares to return home after serving time in prison. Seeking help, she becomes a patient of psychiatrist Dr. Jonathan Banks, whose treatment decisions place him at the centre of an increasingly troubling situation.

As the consequences of a newly prescribed medication unfold, the boundaries between illness, intention and manipulation become uncertain. The investigation that follows challenges the people involved to question what they believe, what they remember and who may be controlling the story. With a carefully constructed mystery and an atmosphere of mounting unease, the film explores trust, professional responsibility and the consequences of decisions made behind closed doors.
