About the Film

Desire, Deception & Dangerous Secrets

Claire Valens is a driven young woman ready to rebuild her life through a promising new position. But her growing attraction to Eve, the wife of her wealthy employer, draws her into a volatile relationship. As hidden motives and unsettling connections to Claire’s past begin to surface, the romance becomes a fight for survival. Directed by Ernie Barbarash, Heatwave blends romantic tension with mystery and thriller elements.
