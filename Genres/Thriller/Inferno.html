Inferno (2016)

About the Film

A Race Against a Global Catastrophe

Inferno (2016) is an action mystery thriller directed by Ron Howard and based on Dan Brown’s bestselling novel. Tom Hanks returns as Harvard symbologist Robert Langdon, who awakens in a Florence hospital with fragmented memories and a disturbing vision. With doctor Sienna Brooks, he follows clues hidden in art, architecture and Dante Alighieri’s enduring poem while trying to uncover the truth behind a plot that threatens millions. The investigation moves through historic European locations, turning cultural landmarks into pieces of a high-stakes puzzle.

Blending conspiracy, historical references, chase sequences and puzzle-solving, the film is the third screen adventure featuring Robert Langdon, after The Da Vinci Code and Angels & Demons.
