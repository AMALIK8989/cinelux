About the Film

A Building Full of Secrets

Sliver (2005), also known as Sliver, is the third entry in the Sliver film series. The story follows Carly Norris, whose inheritance of two valuable diamonds becomes the center of a dispute with her stepfather. When Zeke Hawkins accuses him of rape, detectives Michael Morrison and Kristen Richards begin investigating a case where motives, identities and loyalties are difficult to read. The film builds its mystery around an elaborate plan, competing claims and a series of reversals.
