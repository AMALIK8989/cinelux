About the Film

A Complicated Creative Assignment

Miller's Girl (2024) is a psychological drama thriller written and directed by Jade Halley Bartlett. The story centers on Cairo Sweet, a talented and intellectually ambitious student with a passion for literature and creative writing. When her teacher, Jonathan Miller, assigns a writing project, their interactions develop into a complicated situation that challenges professional boundaries and personal judgment.

As Cairo's writing and the circumstances surrounding the assignment become increasingly intertwined, Jonathan finds himself navigating a difficult personal and professional dilemma. The film explores themes of artistic ambition, interpretation, power, responsibility and the consequences of decisions made when boundaries become uncertain.

Jenna Ortega leads the film as Cairo Sweet, opposite Martin Freeman as Jonathan Miller. The supporting cast includes Bashir Salahuddin, Gideon Adlon, Dagmara Domińczyk and Christine Adams. With its literary references and uneasy character dynamics, Miller's Girl takes a character-focused approach to psychological tension.
