Sister My Sister (1994)

About the Film

A House Divided by Secrets

Adapted from Wendy Kesselman’s play My Sister in This House, Sister My Sister draws on the notorious 1933 Papin sisters case. Christine and Léa are devoted to one another, but their employers’ rigid household rules and the social divisions of 1930s France intensify the tension. Nancy Meckler builds an unsettling portrait of isolation, class, repression and obsession, tracing how everyday domestic pressures can become unbearable.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Set in 1930s France, the film follows sisters Christine and Léa Papin, who work as maids in a wealthy household. Their intense relationship and growing tensions in the home lead to a shocking act of violence.

Nancy Meckler directed Sister My Sister, adapting Wendy Kesselman’s play for the screen.

Wendy Kesselman wrote the screenplay, based on her stage play My Sister in This House.

The principal cast includes Julie Walters, Joely Richardson, Jodhi May and Sophie Thursfield.

The film is inspired by the 1933 Papin sisters murder case in France. It adapts Wendy Kesselman’s dramatic interpretation rather than presenting itself as a documentary.

Sister My Sister was made in 1994 and screened at festivals in the 1994–1995 period; release dates vary by territory.

The commonly listed UK runtime is about 89 minutes. Some US listings report a shorter 86-minute version.

It is generally described as a psychological drama and thriller, with historical and crime elements.
