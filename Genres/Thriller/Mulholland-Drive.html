Mulholland Drive (2001)

About the Film

A Dangerous Inheritance

Mulholland Drive (2001), written and directed by David Lynch, is a surreal mystery thriller set in Los Angeles. After surviving a car crash, an enigmatic woman with amnesia takes refuge in an apartment and meets Betty Elms, an aspiring actress newly arrived in Hollywood. As the women investigate the stranger’s identity, a parallel story follows filmmaker Adam Kesher as he encounters disturbing pressure surrounding his latest casting decision. The film moves through shifting identities, fractured memories and dreamlike images to explore love, ambition, jealousy and the darker mythology of the movie industry.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

The film follows Betty Elms, an aspiring actress newly arrived in Los Angeles, and Rita, a woman with amnesia after a car crash. Their search for Rita’s identity unfolds alongside a director’s increasingly troubling Hollywood experiences.

Mulholland Drive was written and directed by David Lynch.

The film premiered at the Cannes Film Festival on 16 May 2001 and received its US theatrical release on 12 October 2001.

The commonly listed theatrical runtime is approximately 146 minutes.

The principal cast includes Naomi Watts, Laura Harring, Justin Theroux, Ann Miller, Dan Hedaya, Mark Pellegrino and Robert Forster.

Naomi Watts plays Betty Elms and Diane Selwyn, while Laura Harring plays Rita and Camilla Rhodes.

It is commonly described as a mystery thriller and psychological drama, with neo-noir and surrealist elements.

The film’s score was composed by Angelo Badalamenti, whose music contributes to its unsettling, dreamlike atmosphere.
