What the Peeper Saw (1972)

About the Film

A House Filled with Suspicion

What the Peeper Saw (1972), also known by its British release title Night Hair Child and its Italian title La tua presenza nuda!, is a psychological thriller built around an unsettling family mystery. After the death of his first wife, wealthy writer Paul brings his new wife Elise into a secluded household shared with his twelve-year-old son Marcus. Elise becomes increasingly troubled by Marcus’s behaviour and begins to investigate the circumstances surrounding his mother’s death. The film uses shifting perspectives, isolation and uncertainty to create an atmosphere of suspicion.

Directors: James Kelley, Andrea Bianchi
