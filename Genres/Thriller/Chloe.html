Chloe (2009)

About the Film

A Dangerous Game of Desire and Suspicion

Directed by Atom Egoyan and written by Erin Cressida Wilson, Chloe is an erotic psychological thriller about trust, intimacy and the fragile boundaries of a marriage. Catherine Stewart, a successful doctor, becomes increasingly suspicious that her husband David is being unfaithful. Rather than confronting him directly, she makes an unusual decision: she hires Chloe, an escort, to test his fidelity.

What begins as an attempt to confirm or dismiss Catherine's fears gradually becomes something more complicated. Chloe's detailed accounts of her meetings with David affect Catherine in unexpected ways, awakening emotions and desires she has long kept buried. As the three characters become increasingly entangled, the distinction between truth, performance and manipulation begins to blur.

Starring Julianne Moore, Liam Neeson and Amanda Seyfried, the film builds its suspense through shifting perspectives and intimate character dynamics. Adapted from the French film Nathalie..., Chloe examines jealousy, insecurity, attraction and the consequences of trying to control another person's private life.
