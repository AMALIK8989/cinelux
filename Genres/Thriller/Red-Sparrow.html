Red Sparrow (2018)

About the Film

A Spy Caught Between Two Worlds

Red Sparrow (2018) is a spy thriller based on Jason Matthews’ novel of the same name. Jennifer Lawrence stars as Dominika Egorova, a Bolshoi ballerina whose future changes after an injury ends her dancing career. Pressured by circumstances and recruited into the secretive Sparrow School, she is trained to use psychological tactics and manipulation in intelligence operations. Her mission involving CIA officer Nate Nash becomes a tense game of competing loyalties, political secrets and personal risk. Directed by Francis Lawrence and adapted for the screen by Justin Haythe, the film blends espionage, psychological suspense and action.
