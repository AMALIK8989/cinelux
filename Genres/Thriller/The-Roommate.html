About the Film

When a Close Friendship Turns Dangerous

The Roommate (2011) is a psychological thriller directed by Christian E. Christiansen and written by Sonny Mallhi. College freshman Sara Matthews is eager to settle into campus life when she meets Rebecca, her new roommate. The two initially become close, but Rebecca grows increasingly jealous as Sara makes friends and starts a relationship. What first appears to be an intense friendship becomes a disturbing pattern of fixation, manipulation and violence. The film explores the darker side of possessiveness and the danger of mistaking obsession for loyalty.
