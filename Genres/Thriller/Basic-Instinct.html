About the Film

A Dangerous Game of Suspicion and Desire

Basic Instinct (1992) is a neo-noir erotic thriller directed by Paul Verhoeven and written by Joe Eszterhas. The story follows San Francisco homicide detective Nick Curran as he investigates the brutal murder of former rock star Johnny Boz. The investigation points toward Catherine Tramell, a wealthy crime novelist whose latest book appears to echo the killing. As Nick becomes increasingly involved with Catherine, the case grows more complicated and his judgment is tested by desire, mistrust and the possibility that the next move has already been written.

Frequently Asked Questions

Basic Instinct follows San Francisco detective Nick Curran as he investigates the ice-pick murder of a former rock star. The case draws him toward crime novelist Catherine Tramell, whose stories and relationships complicate the investigation.

Basic Instinct was directed by Dutch filmmaker Paul Verhoeven.

The screenplay was written by Joe Eszterhas.

Basic Instinct premiered in Los Angeles on March 18, 1992, and opened in the United States on March 20, 1992.

The commonly listed theatrical runtime is approximately 127 minutes. Some archival sources list different runtimes for particular versions.

The principal cast includes Michael Douglas, Sharon Stone, George Dzundza, Jeanne Tripplehorn, Denis Arndt, Leilani Sarelle and Wayne Knight.

Catherine Tramell is a successful crime novelist who becomes a central suspect in the murder investigation. Her connection to the victim and her unsettling resemblance to a character in her writing make her motives difficult to read.

Basic Instinct is commonly classified as an erotic thriller, psychological mystery and neo-noir crime thriller.
