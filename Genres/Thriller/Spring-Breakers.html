Spring Breakers (2012)

About the Film

A Neon-Lit Descent into Crime

Spring Breakers (2012) is a crime thriller written and directed by Harmony Korine. Best friends Brit, Candy, Cotty and Faith are eager to escape their everyday routines for a week in Florida, but they do not have enough money for the trip. A restaurant robbery gets them there, only for the celebration to end in arrest. Their release comes through Alien, a flamboyant local dealer and aspiring rapper whose world of weapons, money and criminal ambition offers a dangerous version of freedom. As the group fractures and loyalties shift, the film turns the familiar spring-break fantasy into a vivid, unsettling portrait of excess, friendship and consequence.
