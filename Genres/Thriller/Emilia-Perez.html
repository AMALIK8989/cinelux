About the Film

A Reinvention with No Easy Escape

Rita is a talented lawyer whose work largely serves powerful clients looking to escape justice. Her life takes an extraordinary turn when Manitas, a feared cartel boss, hires her to arrange a disappearance and make possible a long-held personal transformation. Years later, the consequences of that decision ripple through the lives of Emilia, Rita, Jessi and Epifanía. Written and directed by Jacques Audiard, Emilia Pérez combines crime drama, musical sequences and operatic storytelling into a provocative portrait of identity, reinvention, loyalty and power.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Emilia Pérez follows a powerful Mexican cartel leader who enlists lawyer Rita to help fake her death and begin a new life as the woman she has always wanted to be. The musical crime drama follows four women navigating identity, ambition, family and danger.

Emilia Pérez was written and directed by French filmmaker Jacques Audiard.

The principal cast includes Karla Sofía Gascón as Emilia Pérez, Zoe Saldaña as Rita, Selena Gomez as Jessi, Adriana Paz as Epifanía, Édgar Ramírez as Gustavo Brun and Mark Ivanir as Dr. Wasserman.

Emilia Pérez premiered in competition at the 2024 Cannes Film Festival. It opened in selected US cinemas on November 1, 2024, before its Netflix release in several territories on November 13, 2024.

The film runs for approximately 130 minutes (2 hours and 10 minutes). Some regional listings may show a slightly different runtime.

The film is primarily in Spanish and also includes English and French dialogue.

Emilia Pérez blends musical, crime and drama elements, with thriller and film-noir influences.

At the 2024 Cannes Film Festival, the film won the Jury Prize and its female ensemble shared the Best Actress award. It also received major awards-season recognition, including Academy Awards.

Netflix distributed the film in several territories and made it available for streaming following its theatrical release.
