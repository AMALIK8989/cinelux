About the Film

A Diary of Obsession

Poison Ivy II: Lily (1996) follows Lily Leonetti, a sheltered art student who moves from Michigan to Los Angeles. In her new apartment, she discovers a box of photographs and a diary left by a former tenant named Ivy. Fascinated by Ivy’s self-assured personality, Lily begins to adopt her style and outlook. As Lily grows closer to fellow student Gredin, her art professor Donald Falk takes an increasingly troubling interest in her, drawing her into a tense story of desire, jealousy and obsession.
