Wild Things 2 (2004)

About the Film

A Fortune Built on Lies

Wild Things 2 is a 2004 American thriller and the second entry in the Wild Things film series. After the death of her wealthy stepfather, Brittney Havers faces an uncertain inheritance. Her classmate Maya King then claims to be the dead man’s illegitimate daughter, and a DNA test appears to support the claim. An insurance investigator begins probing the circumstances surrounding the plane crash, uncovering a web of manipulation, false identities and deadly betrayals. Directed by Jack Perez, the film combines crime, mystery and erotic-thriller elements.
