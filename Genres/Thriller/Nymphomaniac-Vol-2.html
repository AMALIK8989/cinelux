Nymphomaniac: Vol. II (2013)

About the Film

The Story Continues

Nymphomaniac: Vol. II continues the story introduced in Volume I. Joe (Charlotte Gainsbourg) remains in the care of Seligman (Stellan Skarsgård), and their conversations lead into the more difficult chapters of her adult life. As Joe describes her relationships, compulsions and attempts to understand herself, the film explores the tension between desire and consequence, intimacy and isolation, and the stories people tell to make sense of their experiences.

Written and directed by Lars von Trier, the second volume expands the film’s psychological and philosophical themes while bringing Joe’s account toward its unsettling conclusion. The narrative uses Seligman’s observations and interpretations as a counterpoint to Joe’s memories, creating a dialogue about morality, identity, sexuality and personal responsibility. The film is best described as an adult psychological drama rather than a conventional thriller; its inclusion in CineLux’s Thriller collection is a collection placement, not its primary industry genre classification.
