Stoker (2013)

About the Film

A Family Secret Beneath the Surface

Following the sudden death of her father, eighteen-year-old India Stoker is left to navigate a changed family life alongside her emotionally distant mother, Evelyn. At the funeral, she encounters Charlie, a previously unknown uncle whose unexpected arrival brings a new and unsettling presence into their home.

Charlie is charming, attentive and seemingly eager to become part of their lives. India, however, begins to notice strange details about him. As she becomes increasingly fascinated by her uncle, she discovers that the family history she thought she understood may conceal something far more disturbing.

Directed by Park Chan-wook and written by Wentworth Miller, Stoker is a visually striking psychological thriller built around secrecy, obsession, identity and the darker side of family relationships. Its carefully controlled atmosphere and growing tension make it a distinctive entry in modern gothic-tinged thriller cinema.
