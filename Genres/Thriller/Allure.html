Allure (2017)

About the Film

An Intense Bond That Turns Into Obsession

Allure (also known by its original festival title, A Worthy Companion) is a Canadian psychological thriller from directors Carlos and Jason Sanchez. Laura Drake works for her father’s house-cleaning business and struggles with the emotional weight of her past. When she meets Eva, a gifted sixteen-year-old pianist unhappy with the restrictions of her home life, the two form an intense connection. Laura persuades Eva to leave home and live with her, but what first appears to be an escape gradually becomes an unsettling relationship shaped by obsession, manipulation and emotional instability. The film examines codependency, power, isolation and the fragile boundary between protection and control.
