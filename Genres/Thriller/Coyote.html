Coyote (2023)

About the Film

A Dystopian Fight to Find Each Other

Coyote imagines a future in which teleportation offers a route out of genocide for people forced to flee their homes. Ekaterina and Anya attempt to reach safety, but placing their trust in the wrong back-alley teleporter tears them apart. Their efforts to reunite lead into a sinister human-trafficking operation entangled with political corruption. Directed and written by Dustin Curtis Murphy, the film blends science-fiction world-building with thriller tension and a story of forbidden love, displacement and survival.
