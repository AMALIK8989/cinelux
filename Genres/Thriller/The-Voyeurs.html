About the Film

A Window Into Dangerous Secrets

The Voyeurs (2021) is a psychological erotic thriller written and directed by Michael Mohan. Newly settled in a Montréal apartment, Pippa and Thomas notice the couple living directly opposite them and begin watching their private lives through the windows. Their fascination grows into an obsession, and an attempt to interfere in the neighbors’ relationship sets off a chain of revelations with increasingly serious consequences. The film explores voyeurism, privacy, intimacy, deception and the uneasy boundary between observation and participation.
