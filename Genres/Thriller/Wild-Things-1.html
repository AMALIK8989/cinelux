About the Film

Nothing in This Town Is What It Seems

When teen socialite Kelly Van Ryan fails to attract the attention of her guidance counselor, Sam Lombardo, she accuses him of rape — a scandal that gets him suspended and arrested overnight. Sam turns to Ken Bowden, a small-time personal injury lawyer, to clear his name, but the case grows stranger still when a second accuser, troubled local girl Suzie Toller, comes forward to corroborate Kelly's story. As Detective Ray Duquette investigates, he begins to suspect the truth is far more complicated than either version of events — a spiraling, double-crossing scheme of lust, money, and revenge that leaves almost no one in town innocent. Director John McNaughton's steamy, twist-driven neo-noir became a surprise box office hit and has endured as a cult classic of late-'90s erotic thriller cinema.
