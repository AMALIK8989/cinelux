Deep Water (2022)

About the Film

A Dangerous Game of Marriage and Deception

Deep Water is a 2022 erotic psychological thriller directed by Adrian Lyne, adapted from Patricia Highsmith's 1957 novel of the same name. The film explores the unsettling relationship between Vic Van Allen, a wealthy husband, and his wife Melinda, whose open affairs and provocative behaviour place their marriage under constant strain.

Vic appears willing to tolerate Melinda's relationships with other men in order to keep their family together. Yet his calm exterior and unusual responses make those around him increasingly uneasy. When people connected to Melinda begin to disappear, suspicion gathers around Vic, turning their private arrangement into a disturbing mystery.

Set against a humid Southern backdrop, the film examines jealousy, obsession, manipulation, sexual tension and the fragile boundaries between trust and control. Ben Affleck and Ana de Armas lead a cast that brings the Van Allens' complicated world to life.

Directed by Adrian Lyne, whose filmography includes Fatal Attraction and Unfaithful, Deep Water returns to the director's familiar territory of intimate relationships, desire and psychological danger.
