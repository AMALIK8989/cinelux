Oldboy (2013)

About the Film

Twenty Years of Captivity. A Lifetime of Questions.

Joe Doucett is an advertising executive whose life takes a horrifying turn when he is abducted and locked inside a strange room resembling a hotel. He is given food, television and a place to sleep, but no explanation for why he has been taken or who is responsible. His confinement stretches on for twenty years, leaving him isolated from the world and haunted by the loss of his former life.

Without warning, Joe is released. Rather than finding peace, he emerges with a single purpose: to identify the person who imprisoned him and understand the reason behind his punishment. With help from Marie Sebastian, he follows a trail of clues that leads him toward a powerful and deeply unsettling figure from his past.

Directed by Spike Lee and written by Mark Protosevich, Oldboy is an American adaptation of the 2003 South Korean film directed by Park Chan-wook. The 2013 version brings its own cast and interpretation to a story built around revenge, captivity, manipulation and the consequences of secrets.

Starring Josh Brolin, Elizabeth Olsen and Sharlto Copley, the film blends action, mystery and psychological tension into a dark revenge thriller. Its central mystery follows Joe as he tries to uncover not only who controlled his life, but why his release may be part of a much larger plan.
