About the Film

A Chaotic Road Trip Turns Into a Crime Caper

Drive-Away Dolls (2024) is an action-comedy thriller directed by Ethan Coen and written by Coen with Tricia Cooke. The story follows Jamie, an outgoing free spirit dealing with another breakup, and Marian, her more reserved friend who is ready for a change. The pair decide to drive to Tallahassee, but their plans unravel after they pick up a rental car that contains something its intended recipients desperately want back.

As the unlikely duo travel across the American South, they become entangled with a crew of bumbling criminals and a series of increasingly strange encounters. Margaret Qualley and Geraldine Viswanathan lead the film as Jamie and Marian, with Beanie Feldstein, Colman Domingo, Pedro Pascal, Bill Camp and Matt Damon among the supporting cast.

Blending road-movie energy, crime-comedy complications and thriller elements, Drive-Away Dolls uses the contrast between its two leads to fuel an unpredictable journey. The film was released in 2024 and runs for approximately 84 minutes.
