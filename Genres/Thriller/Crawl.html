Crawl (2019)

About the Film

A Hurricane. A Flooded House. A Deadly Hunt.

Crawl (2019) is a survival horror thriller directed by Alexandre Aja. Set in Florida during a catastrophic hurricane, the film follows competitive swimmer Haley Keller as she defies evacuation orders to search for her father, Dave. She discovers him badly injured in the crawl space beneath their family home. With the storm intensifying and floodwater rapidly rising, Haley and Dave find themselves trapped in a confined, submerged environment where hungry alligators are closing in. The film combines disaster-movie tension, creature-feature scares and a father-daughter survival story.

Starring Kaya Scodelario and Barry Pepper, Crawl builds suspense through its tight setting, rising water and escalating animal attacks. Alexandre Aja directs from a screenplay by Michael and Shawn Rasmussen, with Sam Raimi among the producers.
