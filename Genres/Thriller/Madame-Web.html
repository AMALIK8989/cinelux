About the Film

A Future Written in the Web

Directed by S. J. Clarkson, Madame Web reimagines one of Marvel’s most enigmatic characters in a standalone origin story. Cassandra Webb is a New York paramedic whose emerging clairvoyant abilities reveal fragments of what may happen next. When she crosses paths with Julia Cornwall, Anya Corazón and Mattie Franklin, Cassandra discovers that their futures—and her own hidden history—are connected. With Ezekiel Sims hunting the young women, she must decide how far she will go to prevent a deadly future. The film blends superhero mythology with suspense, action and science-fiction elements.
