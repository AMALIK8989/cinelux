The Substance (2024)

About the Film

A Terrifying Reflection of Beauty and Identity

The Substance is a 2024 body horror thriller written and directed by Coralie Fargeat. The film follows Elisabeth Sparkle, a once-celebrated television personality whose career is threatened when the entertainment industry begins treating her as past her prime.

After losing her place in the spotlight, Elisabeth discovers a mysterious black-market treatment known as the Substance. The experimental formula allows her to create a younger version of herself, Sue, who appears to possess everything Elisabeth believes she has lost: youth, beauty and opportunity.

The transformation comes with one essential condition. Elisabeth and Sue must share their existence in carefully divided periods of time. What initially appears to be an extraordinary second chance gradually becomes a disturbing struggle involving identity, vanity, obsession and control.

Featuring striking performances from Demi Moore, Margaret Qualley and Dennis Quaid, The Substance uses grotesque physical transformation, dark humour and psychological tension to examine society's fixation on youth and its treatment of women's bodies.

The film premiered in competition at the 2024 Cannes Film Festival, where Coralie Fargeat received the award for Best Screenplay. Its distinctive combination of science fiction, horror and social satire makes it one of the most recognisable body horror films of 2024.
