Jailbait (2014)

About the Film

Innocent. Guilty. Trapped.

Jailbait is a 2014 crime drama thriller directed by Jared Cohn. The story follows Anna Nix after a violent confrontation with her abusive stepfather lands her in a juvenile prison. The institution brings its own dangers: shifting alliances between inmates, gang pressure, drugs, exploitation and authority figures who abuse their power. As Anna struggles to survive, she is forced to confront trauma, difficult choices and the possibility of redemption.

Rather than presenting prison as a simple backdrop, the film places Anna inside a hostile environment where trust is scarce and survival often means making uncomfortable decisions. Its central conflict combines a coming-of-age story with crime and thriller elements, focusing on the cost of violence and the fight to regain control of one’s life.
