About the Film

Camp Nightwing, 1978: The Killings Before the Curse Had a Name

Fourteen years before Deena's night of terror, sisters Cindy and Ziggy Berman spend the summer of 1978 as camper and counselor at Camp Nightwing, split down the middle between kids from wealthy Sunnyvale and staff from cursed Shadyside. When fellow counselor Tommy Slater is possessed by an ancient evil and turns his axe on everyone in his path, the sisters must set aside their rivalry to survive the night — and uncover the truth behind the witch's curse that has doomed Shadyside for three centuries.
