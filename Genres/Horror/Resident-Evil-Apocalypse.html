About the Film

Raccoon City Falls, and Nemesis Rises

Days after the outbreak at The Hive, Raccoon City itself has fallen — its streets crawling with the infected as the Umbrella Corporation seals the city under quarantine to contain the disaster. Alice wakes up with enhanced strength and senses, the result of Umbrella's experiments on her, and falls in with a ragtag group of survivors: S.T.A.R.S. officer Jill Valentine, mercenary Carlos Olivera, and a police sergeant desperate to find his kidnapped daughter. As the group races against a midnight deadline before Umbrella firebombs the entire city, they're hunted by Nemesis, a hulking new bioweapon built from a mutated human host and armed with a rocket launcher, with orders to eliminate every remaining member of S.T.A.R.S.
