About the Film

A Shaman's Bloodline, and a Presence That Isn't the Goddess

Presented as a documentary, The Medium follows a film crew profiling Nim, a hereditary shaman in Thailand's Isan region who has inherited the mantle of the goddess Bayan through generations of women in her family. When Nim's niece Mink begins to show disturbing symptoms after a family funeral, everyone assumes the guardian spirit has simply chosen its next vessel — until Mink's behavior grows violent and erratic in ways no one recognizes. As the crew keeps filming, it becomes clear that whatever has taken hold of Mink did not come from the goddess at all, and the family's own hidden history may be the reason it got in.
