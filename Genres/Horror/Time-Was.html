About the Film

A House That Won't Let Go

Beth takes on the DIY renovation of a house she plans to flip, and soon realises she is in over her head. She enlists Melissa, a designer, to help her, and their working relationship turns into an attraction. As eerie disturbances in the house escalate, Beth begins to question her sanity and what is real.

Written and directed by Jade Winters, the film is set largely in one central location and follows four main cast members. It was produced by Wicked Winters Films, with Alex Hogben as producer and David Frederick on music.
