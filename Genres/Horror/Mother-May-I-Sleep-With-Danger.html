About the Film

A New Love, a Mother's Instinct, and a Secret That Bites Back

Theater major Leah brings her new girlfriend Pearl home to meet her mother Julie, who is caught off guard when Pearl arrives at the door. Julie tries to embrace the relationship for Leah's sake, but she can't shake a growing unease about the mysterious young woman her daughter has fallen for. As Julie digs deeper, her suspicions lead to a startling discovery: Pearl is the leader of a secret, seductive vampire coven, and Leah is already in far more danger than she realizes. A lesbian vampire reimagining of the 1996 Lifetime cult classic, produced from a story by James Franco.
