Resident Evil: Degeneration (2008)

About the Film

Outbreak at Harvardville Airport

Resident Evil: Degeneration (2008), also known as Biohazard: Degeneration, is a feature-length CGI animated film based on Capcom’s Resident Evil franchise. Set seven years after the Raccoon City incident, the story begins when an infected individual triggers a zombie outbreak at Harvardville Airport. Leon S. Kennedy and Claire Redfield reunite as the crisis escalates, uncovering links to WilPharma, the G-Virus and a survivor seeking revenge. Directed by Makoto Kamiya and written by Shotaro Suga, the film blends survival horror, action and science-fiction elements within the continuity of the game series.
