Resident Evil: Death Island (2023)

About the Film

Five Familiar Survivors. One Island of Nightmares.

Resident Evil: Death Island brings several familiar faces from the survival-horror series together for a feature-length CG animated mission. In San Francisco, Jill Valentine is responding to a zombie outbreak linked to a new T-virus, while Leon Kennedy pursues kidnappers who have taken scientist Dr. Antonio Taylor. Claire Redfield is investigating a mysterious creature attacking whales in the bay. Their separate cases begin to converge, and Chris Redfield and Rebecca Chambers join the investigation as the trail leads to Alcatraz Island. Directed by Eiichiro Hasumi and written by Makoto Fukami, the film combines the franchise's bioterror conspiracies, infected creatures and returning heroes in a story set within the Resident Evil universe.
