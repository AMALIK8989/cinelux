Resident Evil: Damnation (2012)

About the Film

Leon Kennedy in a B.O.W. War Zone

Resident Evil: Damnation (also known as Biohazard: Damnation) is a 2012 computer-animated feature set in the Resident Evil universe. The story follows DSO agent Leon S. Kennedy as he infiltrates a former Soviet republic to investigate the use of bio-organic weapons during a civil war. Despite orders to evacuate, Leon remains in the country and works to uncover the truth behind the B.O.W. deployment. The film combines military action, creature horror and the series’ survival-horror mythology, while bringing familiar characters including Ada Wong and Ingrid Hunnigan into the mission.
