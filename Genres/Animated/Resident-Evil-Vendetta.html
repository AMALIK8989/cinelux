Resident Evil: Vendetta (2017)

About the Film

A Deadly Mission Against Bioterrorism

Resident Evil: Vendetta (2017) is a feature-length computer-generated animated action-horror film set in the world of Capcom’s Resident Evil series. After an operation involving a mansion and a dangerous viral outbreak, BSAA captain Chris Redfield crosses paths with Leon S. Kennedy and Professor Rebecca Chambers. Their investigation points to Glenn Arias, a powerful arms dealer seeking revenge through a devastating bioterror attack in New York. Directed by Takanori Tsujimoto and written by Makoto Fukami, the film brings together familiar game characters in a standalone story built around zombies, bio-organic weapons and high-stakes action.
